Agricultural Insurance & Risk Management

Promoting awareness of agricultural risks and encouraging responsible approaches that can help farmers and agribusinesses manage uncertainty and protect their agricultural investments.

Managing Agricultural Risks

Agriculture is exposed to different risks, including weather events, pests, diseases, market changes and other unexpected challenges. Understanding these risks can help farmers make better decisions.

🌧️

Weather Risks

Flooding, drought and other weather conditions can affect agricultural production and farm income.

🐛

Pests & Diseases

Crop pests and livestock diseases can reduce productivity and cause financial losses.

📉

Market Risks

Changes in prices, demand and market conditions can affect the profitability of agricultural businesses.

🔥

Production Risks

Production challenges and unexpected events can affect farm output and agricultural investments.

🛡️

Insurance Awareness

Agricultural insurance may provide eligible farmers with financial protection against specified covered risks.

📊

Risk Planning

Proper planning can help farmers identify risks and develop suitable strategies for managing them.

Risk Management Strategies

Diversification

Farmers can consider appropriate diversification of crops, livestock or agricultural activities to reduce dependence on one source of income.

Good Farm Practices

Appropriate production practices can help reduce avoidable risks and improve farm resilience.

Record Keeping

Keeping accurate farm and financial records can support better planning and decision-making.

Insurance

Eligible farmers may consider appropriate agricultural insurance products offered by authorized providers.

Emergency Planning

Preparing for possible emergencies can help farmers respond more effectively when unexpected events occur.

Market Planning

Understanding market conditions and planning sales can help reduce exposure to avoidable market risks.

Before Taking Agricultural Insurance

1. Understand the Policy

Carefully read and understand the insurance policy, including the risks covered and the conditions attached.

2. Check Eligibility

Confirm that the farm, crop, livestock or agricultural activity meets the requirements of the insurance product.

3. Understand the Premium

Understand the amount payable, payment schedule and other financial obligations before entering into an insurance agreement.

4. Know the Claims Process

Applicants should understand how and when claims must be reported and what documentation may be required.

5. Use Authorized Providers

Farmers should verify that an insurance provider and product are properly authorized before making payments or signing agreements.

Important Notice: Insurance products, eligibility requirements, premiums, coverage and claims procedures vary by provider and programme. Farmers should verify all current information directly with the relevant authorized provider or official government programme before making financial commitments.

Building Resilient Agriculture

Better risk awareness and responsible planning can help farmers, cooperatives and agribusinesses prepare for uncertainty and build a stronger agricultural sector in Kogi State.
