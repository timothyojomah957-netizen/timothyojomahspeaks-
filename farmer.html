Supporting the Farmers of Kogi State

Agriculture is central to the economic and social development of Kogi State. The Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security is committed to supporting farmers, strengthening agricultural productivity and creating opportunities throughout the agricultural value chain.

Our Areas of Support

🌾

Farmer Empowerment

Promoting opportunities and initiatives that help farmers improve their productivity, income and participation in agriculture.

🚜

Agricultural Support

Supporting agricultural development through improved practices, knowledge sharing and access to relevant agricultural opportunities.

👨‍🌾

Youth in Agriculture

Encouraging young people to see agriculture as a viable career and business opportunity.

🌱

Food Production

Promoting increased agricultural production and initiatives that contribute to food security across Kogi State.

Agricultural Development Focus

🌾 Crop Production

Supporting initiatives aimed at improving crop production and agricultural productivity.

🐄 Livestock Development

Encouraging sustainable livestock production and development opportunities for farmers.

👨‍🌾 Farmer Training

Promoting knowledge, skills and modern agricultural practices among farmers.

🌱 Sustainable Agriculture

Encouraging responsible agricultural practices that support long-term food production and environmental sustainability.
