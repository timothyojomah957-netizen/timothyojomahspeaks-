How Farmers Can Seek Support

1. Identify Your Need

Determine the type of agricultural information, service, training or support you require.

2. Check Available Programs

Review official Ministry programs, services and opportunities to identify those relevant to your needs.

3. Contact the Ministry

Contact the Ministry through the official contact channels for further information and guidance.

4. Follow Official Requirements

Where a program or service has eligibility requirements, applicants should follow the official instructions and provide the required information.

Important: Availability of specific farmer support programs, applications, agricultural inputs, training opportunities and other services may vary. Always confirm current information through official Ministry channels.

Need Assistance?

Farmers seeking information about agricultural programs, services, opportunities or other Ministry activities can contact the Ministry directly.
