Pest & Disease Awareness

Promoting early observation, responsible prevention and timely reporting of agricultural pests and diseases to protect crops, livestock and food production.

Protecting Farms Through Awareness

Early identification of unusual crop damage or signs of animal illness can help farmers respond quickly and seek appropriate professional assistance.

Why Awareness Matters

Agricultural pests and diseases can affect crop growth, livestock health, farm income and food availability. Regular monitoring and good farm hygiene are important parts of responsible agricultural management.


Farmers should avoid making assumptions about the cause of a problem. When symptoms are unclear or serious, seek advice from qualified agricultural extension or veterinary professionals.

Common Areas of Awareness

Farmers should regularly observe their farms and report unusual problems through appropriate agricultural channels.

🐛

Crop Pests

Insects and other organisms can damage leaves, stems, roots, fruits and stored agricultural products.

🌿

Crop Diseases

Diseases can cause unusual spots, wilting, discoloration, poor growth or other changes in crops.

🐄

Livestock Diseases

Changes in animal behaviour, appetite, movement or general condition should be taken seriously.

📦

Stored Product Pests

Proper storage and regular inspection can help reduce damage to harvested crops and food products.

🌱

Farm Hygiene

Good sanitation and responsible farm management can help reduce conditions that encourage some agricultural problems.

📢

Early Reporting

Reporting unusual outbreaks quickly can help agricultural authorities and professionals assess the situation appropriately.

What Farmers Should Do

1

Inspect Farms Regularly

Check crops, livestock, equipment and storage areas regularly for unusual changes or signs of damage.

2

Record Unusual Symptoms

Where possible, note the affected crop or animal, location, date and visible symptoms to help professionals assess the problem.

3

Seek Professional Advice

Contact appropriate agricultural extension officers or veterinary professionals when serious or unfamiliar symptoms appear.

4

Follow Approved Recommendations

Use agricultural treatments and control measures only according to appropriate professional or product-label guidance.

5

Report Suspected Outbreaks

Farmers should promptly report suspected serious outbreaks through authorized agricultural or veterinary channels.

Signs to Watch For

🍃 Leaf Damage

Holes, unusual spots, discoloration or unexpected changes in leaves.

🌱 Poor Growth

Stunted growth, wilting or unexpected changes in plant development.

🪰 Unusual Insects

Unexpected increases in insects or visible pest activity around crops.

🐄 Animal Changes

Changes in appetite, movement, behaviour or general condition.

🌾 Storage Damage

Insects, damaged grains, mould or unusual deterioration in stored products.

📉 Sudden Losses

Unexpected crop or livestock losses should be investigated promptly.

Important Safety Notice

Do not use pesticides, veterinary medicines or other agricultural chemicals based only on an internet description of symptoms. Correct identification and professional guidance are important for safe and effective control.

Protecting Agriculture Through Early Action

Awareness, regular monitoring, responsible farm management and timely professional assistance can help farmers protect crops, livestock and agricultural livelihoods.
