Women in Agriculture

Promoting the participation of women in agricultural production, agribusiness, processing, value addition and sustainable agricultural development across Kogi State.

Empowering Women in Agriculture

Women play an important role in agriculture and food systems. Supporting women with knowledge, skills, opportunities and access to agricultural value chains can contribute to stronger families, communities and the wider agricultural sector.

🌾

Crop Production

Women can participate in crop production and develop sustainable agricultural enterprises.

🐄

Livestock

Opportunities exist for women in livestock production, animal management and related agricultural businesses.

🐟

Fisheries & Aquaculture

Women can participate in fish production, aquaculture, processing and fisheries-related value chains.

🏭

Agro-Processing

Agricultural processing and value addition provide opportunities for women to develop businesses and create additional value.

💼

Women Agribusiness

Women entrepreneurs can participate in agricultural marketing, distribution, processing and other agribusiness activities.

📚

Training & Skills

Training and capacity building can help women develop agricultural, technical and business skills.

Key Areas of Support

Capacity Building

Promoting access to agricultural knowledge, training and skills development.

Cooperatives

Encouraging women to participate in farmer groups and cooperatives for collaboration and shared opportunities.

Market Access

Strengthening connections between women producers, processors, businesses and agricultural markets.

Value Addition

Encouraging the processing and packaging of agricultural products to increase their value.

Entrepreneurship

Supporting women interested in building sustainable agricultural enterprises and businesses.

Innovation

Encouraging women to explore technology and innovative approaches to agricultural production and business.

Getting Started

1. Identify Your Agricultural Interest

Choose an area such as crop production, livestock, fisheries, processing, marketing or agribusiness.

2. Build Your Skills

Seek relevant agricultural training, practical experience and business knowledge.

3. Explore Opportunities

Check official Ministry programs, agricultural opportunities and partnerships that may be relevant to you.

4. Connect With Other Women

Participation in agricultural groups, associations and cooperatives can create opportunities for collaboration and knowledge sharing.
