Agricultural Statistics & Information

Supporting evidence-based agricultural planning through the collection, organization and use of reliable agricultural information and statistics.

Agricultural Information

Reliable information helps government, farmers, investors, researchers and development partners understand agricultural activities and make better decisions.

📊

Production Data

Information relating to agricultural production can support planning, monitoring and policy development.

🌾

Crop Information

Agricultural information can help identify important crops, production trends and opportunities within the sector.

🐄

Livestock Information

Data relating to livestock production can support planning and development of livestock-related initiatives.

👨‍🌾

Farmer Information

Information about farmers and agricultural communities can support better targeting of agricultural programs and services.

🏪

Market Information

Agricultural market information can help producers and businesses understand market conditions and opportunities.

📈

Trends & Analysis

Agricultural trends and analysis can help stakeholders understand changes in production and the wider agricultural sector.

Uses of Agricultural Statistics

Policy Planning

Reliable data can support the development and review of agricultural policies and strategies.

Program Planning

Statistics can help identify agricultural needs and guide the planning of programs and interventions.

Investment Decisions

Agricultural information can help investors understand opportunities and challenges within the sector.

Research

Researchers can use reliable agricultural information to study trends, challenges and opportunities.

Monitoring

Data can help stakeholders monitor agricultural programs and assess progress toward development objectives.

Public Information

Appropriate agricultural information can improve public understanding of the agricultural sector.

Important Agricultural Data Areas

Crop Production

Information on crop production, farming activities and agricultural output can support sector planning.

Livestock Production

Livestock-related information can contribute to planning for animal production and related value chains.

Agricultural Markets

Market information can support understanding of agricultural prices, demand, supply and market opportunities.

Farmland & Resources

Information about agricultural land and natural resources can support responsible agricultural development.

Farmer Organizations

Information about farmer groups and cooperatives can support agricultural program coordination and outreach.

Data Notice: Agricultural statistics should be interpreted according to their source, collection method, date and coverage. Official figures should be obtained from the appropriate government or authorized statistical sources.

Information for Better Agricultural Decisions

Reliable agricultural information strengthens planning, improves decision-making and supports the development of a more productive and sustainable agricultural sector in Kogi State.
