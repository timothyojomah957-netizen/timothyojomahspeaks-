Agricultural Extension Services

Connecting farmers with agricultural knowledge, improved farming practices, technical information and support for sustainable agricultural development across Kogi State.

What Is Agricultural Extension?

Agricultural extension services help transfer useful agricultural knowledge, technologies and information to farmers. Extension services can help farmers make informed decisions, improve farm management and adopt appropriate agricultural practices.

🌱

Crop Advisory

Information on crop production, planting practices, farm management, harvesting and improved agricultural techniques.

🐄

Livestock Support

Agricultural information relating to livestock production, animal management and improved husbandry practices.

🐟

Fisheries & Aquaculture

Information and guidance relating to fish farming, aquaculture management and responsible fisheries practices.

🚜

Modern Farming Techniques

Promoting awareness of appropriate technologies, improved farming methods and responsible agricultural practices.

📚

Farmer Education

Supporting farmer learning through agricultural training, demonstrations, workshops and knowledge-sharing activities.

📈

Farm Productivity

Providing useful knowledge that can contribute to improved productivity, efficiency and sustainable agricultural development.

Extension Activities

Agricultural extension activities can take place through training, field demonstrations, farmer meetings, technical guidance and community engagement.

Farmer Training

Training activities can provide farmers with practical agricultural knowledge and improved production techniques.

Field Demonstrations

Demonstrations can help farmers understand how improved agricultural practices and technologies work in real farming situations.

Technical Information

Farmers can receive information relevant to crop production, livestock, fisheries, farm management and agricultural value chains.

Community Engagement

Extension activities can connect agricultural officers with farmers, communities and other agricultural stakeholders.

Farmer Groups

Extension activities may be organized through farmer associations, cooperatives and community-based agricultural groups.

Agricultural Innovation

Farmers can learn about appropriate innovations and technologies that may improve agricultural production and business activities.

How Farmers Can Access Extension Information

Farmers who need agricultural information can follow these general steps to seek appropriate guidance.

1. Identify Your Need

Identify the agricultural challenge, production issue or information you need assistance with.

2. Contact the Appropriate Office

Contact the relevant agricultural office, extension personnel or appropriate Ministry channel for guidance.

3. Participate in Training

Where available, participate in agricultural training, workshops, demonstrations and farmer meetings.

4. Apply Appropriate Knowledge

Apply relevant and appropriate agricultural practices to improve farm management and productivity.

5. Keep Learning

Continue seeking reliable agricultural information and stay informed about new farming practices and opportunities.

Important Information for Farmers

Farmers should verify agricultural advice, programmes, funding opportunities, input distribution and other official announcements through appropriate Ministry channels before taking action.
