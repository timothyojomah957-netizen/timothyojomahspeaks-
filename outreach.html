Agricultural Outreach

Connecting farmers, communities and agricultural stakeholders with information, services, training and opportunities that support agricultural development across Kogi State.

Our Outreach Activities

Agricultural outreach helps bring useful knowledge and services closer to farmers and rural communities while creating opportunities for dialogue and collaboration.

👨‍🌾

Farmer Outreach

Reaching farmers with information on improved agricultural practices, programs, services and available opportunities.

🎓

Training & Workshops

Organizing agricultural learning activities to improve knowledge, practical skills and farm management capacity.

🏘️

Community Engagement

Engaging rural communities to understand local agricultural needs and encourage participation in development initiatives.

🌾

Field Demonstrations

Promoting practical demonstrations that help farmers understand improved production methods and technologies.

🤝

Stakeholder Engagement

Connecting government institutions, farmers, businesses, communities and development partners.

📢

Agricultural Awareness

Sharing important agricultural information and raising awareness about opportunities and responsible farming practices.

Outreach Approach

1. Identify Community Needs

Understanding the challenges and priorities of farmers and agricultural communities.

2. Share Relevant Information

Providing practical information that can help farmers improve production, management and marketing.

3. Build Local Capacity

Supporting farmers and community members through training, knowledge-sharing and practical learning.

4. Encourage Participation

Creating opportunities for farmers, youth, women, cooperatives and other stakeholders to participate in agricultural development.

5. Follow Up

Maintaining communication with communities and using feedback to improve agricultural outreach activities.

Bringing Agriculture Closer to the People

Through effective outreach and engagement, the Ministry continues to support stronger connections between agricultural institutions, farmers and communities across Kogi State.
