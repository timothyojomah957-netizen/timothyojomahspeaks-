Climate-Smart Agriculture

Promoting farming practices that help farmers manage climate-related challenges, protect agricultural resources and build resilient production systems.

What Is Climate-Smart Agriculture?

Climate-smart agriculture focuses on practical approaches that can help farmers maintain or improve agricultural productivity while managing climate-related risks and protecting natural resources.

🌦️

Climate Information

Farmers can use reliable weather and climate information to support decisions about planting, farm management and harvesting.

🌱

Healthy Soils

Good soil management can help maintain soil productivity and improve the ability of farms to withstand environmental pressures.

💧

Water Management

Efficient use and management of water can help farmers respond to periods of water shortage and changing rainfall patterns.

🌾

Resilient Crops

Farmers can consider suitable crop varieties and production practices that are appropriate for local conditions.

🌳

Environmental Protection

Responsible management of land, vegetation, soil and water can support sustainable agricultural production.

🚜

Efficient Technology

Appropriate technologies and improved farming methods can help farmers use available resources more efficiently.

Climate-Smart Farming Practices

Crop Rotation

Rotating suitable crops can support soil management and help reduce some production risks.

Mulching

Appropriate mulching can help conserve soil moisture and protect soil from erosion.

Water Conservation

Efficient water-use practices can help farmers manage available water resources responsibly.

Agroforestry

Appropriate integration of trees with farming systems can contribute to soil protection and sustainable land management.

Soil Conservation

Practices that reduce erosion and maintain soil quality can support long-term agricultural productivity.

Improved Farm Planning

Farmers can use available climate and agricultural information when planning seasonal farm activities.

Benefits to Farmers

Better Risk Management

Climate-smart practices can help farmers prepare for weather-related challenges and reduce avoidable production risks.

Improved Resource Management

Responsible use of soil, water and other farm resources can support sustainable production.

Farm Resilience

Appropriate farming practices can help agricultural systems become more resilient to changing environmental conditions.

Sustainable Production

Climate-smart agriculture encourages farming approaches that consider both current production and future agricultural needs.

Important Notice: Farmers should seek advice from qualified agricultural extension personnel when selecting climate-smart practices. The most suitable approach may differ depending on location, soil, crop, water availability and other local conditions.

Building Climate-Resilient Agriculture

Climate-smart agriculture can help strengthen the resilience of farmers and agricultural communities while supporting sustainable food production in Kogi State.
