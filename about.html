ABOUT THE COMMISSIONER

Hon. Timothy Ojomah

Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Kogi State

Hon. Timothy Ojomah is the Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security in Kogi State, serving under the administration of His Excellency, Alhaji Ahmed Usman Ododo, Governor of Kogi State.

As Commissioner, Hon. Timothy Ojomah provides leadership and strategic direction for the agricultural sector, with emphasis on food security, agricultural productivity, modern farming practices, youth empowerment and the development of sustainable agricultural value chains.

The Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security continues to support initiatives aimed at improving food production, strengthening farmers' access to agricultural inputs and mechanisation, creating employment opportunities and encouraging young people to participate in modern agriculture.

The Commissioner also promotes agricultural investment and sustainable livelihoods as important drivers of economic development in Kogi State.

VISION

To promote a productive, modern and sustainable agricultural sector that contributes significantly to food security, employment creation and economic growth in Kogi State.

KEY AREAS OF FOCUS

  • Food security and increased agricultural production
  • Support for farmers and agricultural cooperatives
  • Agricultural mechanisation
  • Youth empowerment and training
  • Development of agricultural value chains
  • Promotion of private-sector investment in agriculture
  • Sustainable and modern farming practices
  • Employment creation through agriculture

COMMITMENT TO KOGI STATE

Hon. Timothy Ojomah remains committed to advancing the agricultural sector and supporting the farmers and people of Kogi State through programmes that promote food production, economic opportunities and sustainable agricultural development.
