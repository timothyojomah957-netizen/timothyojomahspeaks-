Agricultural Advisory Services

Providing farmers and agricultural stakeholders with useful knowledge, guidance and information to support better agricultural decisions and improved farm productivity.

Our Advisory Services

Agricultural advisory services help farmers access practical information and guidance on production, farm management, marketing and other agricultural activities.

🌾

Crop Production Advice

Providing guidance on crop production practices, farm management and improved agricultural techniques.

🐄

Livestock Guidance

Supporting livestock farmers with information on animal management, production practices and farm improvement.

🌱

Soil Management

Sharing information on responsible soil management and practices that can help maintain agricultural productivity.

💧

Water Management

Providing guidance on efficient agricultural water use and practices that support sustainable production.

📈

Farm Business Advice

Supporting farmers with information on farm planning, record keeping, marketing and agricultural business management.

🤝

Market Information

Connecting farmers with useful information about agricultural markets, value chains and potential business opportunities.

How Farmers Can Benefit

Better Farm Decisions

Access to useful agricultural information can help farmers make more informed decisions about their farming activities.

Improved Productivity

Practical knowledge and improved production methods can contribute to more efficient and productive farming.

Access to Opportunities

Advisory services can help farmers become aware of relevant agricultural programs, training and opportunities.

Stronger Farm Management

Farmers can gain useful knowledge about planning, record keeping, resource management and agricultural business practices.

Knowledge Sharing

Advisory activities encourage communication and knowledge exchange between farmers, extension personnel and agricultural stakeholders.

Supporting Farmers With Knowledge

The Ministry is committed to strengthening agricultural knowledge, information sharing and advisory support to help farmers and communities contribute to the growth of Kogi State's agricultural sector.
