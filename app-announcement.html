Agricultural Loan and Financing Opportunities

Public Announcement

Farmers, agricultural cooperatives, agribusiness owners, women and young people involved in agriculture are invited to apply for available agricultural financing opportunities.

The programme is designed to support agricultural production, livestock farming, farm equipment, agro-processing, marketing and other approved agricultural activities.

Who Can Apply?

  • Smallholder farmers
  • Commercial farmers
  • Agricultural cooperatives
  • Women in agriculture
  • Youth in agriculture
  • Agribusiness owners
  • Agro-processors and agricultural entrepreneurs

Areas Supported

  • Crop production
  • Livestock production
  • Poultry farming
  • Fish farming
  • Farm machinery and equipment
  • Irrigation
  • Agro-processing
  • Storage and agricultural marketing

Basic Requirements

  • Valid means of identification
  • Active telephone number
  • Farm or agribusiness information
  • Farm location
  • Details of the proposed agricultural project
  • Bank account details where required
  • Other documents specified by the financing programme

Application Deadline

Application Deadline: To be announced

Applicants are encouraged to submit their applications early and ensure that all required information is complete.

Important Notice

Submission of an application does not automatically guarantee approval. Applications will be reviewed according to the eligibility criteria and conditions of the applicable financing programme.

Applicants should provide accurate information and should not make payments to individuals claiming to guarantee loan approval.
