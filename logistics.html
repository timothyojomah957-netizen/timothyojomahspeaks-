Why Agricultural Logistics Matters

Reduce Delays

Efficient transportation can help agricultural products reach storage, processing and markets on time.

Reduce Losses

Proper handling and transportation can help reduce damage and unnecessary post-harvest losses.

Connect Farmers to Markets

Better logistics can help farmers reach buyers and agricultural markets more efficiently.

Support Food Supply

Reliable movement of agricultural commodities contributes to stronger food supply systems.

Support Agribusiness

Efficient logistics can help agricultural businesses move inputs and finished products.

Create Opportunities

Agricultural logistics can support businesses and employment across the agricultural value chain.

Good Agricultural Transportation Practices

1. Use Suitable Vehicles

Transportation equipment should be appropriate for the type and quantity of agricultural product being moved.

2. Protect Products

Agricultural products should be protected from excessive heat, moisture, contamination and physical damage where necessary.

3. Handle Products Carefully

Proper loading, unloading and handling can help maintain product quality.

4. Plan Routes

Appropriate route planning can help reduce unnecessary delays and transportation costs.

5. Maintain Vehicles

Proper vehicle maintenance can improve safety and reliability when transporting agricultural products.

Strengthening Agricultural Value Chains

Better transportation and logistics can help connect farmers, processors, businesses and consumers while supporting a stronger agricultural economy in Kogi State.
