Agriculture Data

Providing agricultural information and data that can support farmers, agricultural planning, research, investment and food security across Kogi State.

Agricultural Data Areas

Reliable agricultural information can help government agencies, farmers, researchers, investors and other stakeholders make informed decisions about agriculture and food production.

🌾

Crop Production

Agricultural information relating to crop production, farming activities and major agricultural commodities.

🐄

Livestock

Information relating to livestock production, animal husbandry and livestock-related agricultural activities.

🐟

Fisheries

Information about fisheries, aquaculture and other aquatic agricultural activities.

👨‍🌾

Farmers

Farmer-related information can support agricultural planning, programmes and communication with farming communities.

💰

Markets

Agricultural market information can help stakeholders understand production, marketing and value-chain opportunities.

🌍

Land & Environment

Agricultural planning can consider land resources, environmental conditions and sustainable farming practices.

Uses of Agricultural Data

Government Planning

Agricultural data can support planning, programme development and decision-making within the agricultural sector.

Farmer Support

Reliable information can help agricultural programmes better understand farmer needs and production conditions.

Research

Researchers can use appropriate agricultural information to study farming systems, production and food security.

Investment

Agricultural information can help investors and businesses understand opportunities within agricultural value chains.

Food Security

Agricultural data can contribute to understanding food production and supporting food-security planning.

Policy Development

Evidence-based information can support the development of appropriate agricultural policies and programmes.

Why Agricultural Data Matters

Better Decision-Making

Reliable information helps agricultural stakeholders make more informed decisions about farming, programmes and investments.

Understanding Farmer Needs

Farmer information can help identify challenges, opportunities and support requirements within farming communities.

Monitoring Agricultural Activities

Appropriate data can help stakeholders monitor agricultural activities and evaluate programmes.

Supporting Development

Agricultural information can contribute to long-term planning and development of the agricultural sector.

Important Information

Agricultural statistics and information published on this website should be verified through appropriate official sources before being used for formal research, financial decisions or government reporting.
