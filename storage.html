Agricultural Storage & Warehousing

Promoting better storage, preservation and handling of agricultural products to reduce post-harvest losses, protect food quality and strengthen agricultural value chains across Kogi State.

Protecting Agricultural Products

Proper storage is an important part of agriculture. Good storage practices can help farmers and businesses protect agricultural commodities after harvest and maintain their quality until they reach the market or consumers.

🏬

Warehouses

Suitable warehouses can provide secure spaces for storing agricultural commodities before distribution or sale.

🌽

Grain Storage

Proper grain storage can help protect crops from moisture, pests and other factors that may reduce quality.

🥔

Crop Preservation

Appropriate preservation methods can help extend the useful life of selected agricultural products.

📦

Packaging

Appropriate packaging can help protect agricultural products during storage, transportation and marketing.

❄️

Cold Storage

Cold-storage systems can be useful for appropriate perishable agricultural products where suitable facilities are available.

🚛

Post-Harvest Handling

Careful handling and transportation can reduce unnecessary damage and losses after agricultural products are harvested.

Benefits of Proper Storage

Reduce Post-Harvest Losses

Proper storage can help reduce losses caused by pests, moisture, poor handling and unsuitable storage conditions.

Maintain Quality

Good storage practices can help preserve the quality and condition of agricultural products.

Improve Food Availability

Effective storage can help agricultural products remain available beyond the immediate harvest period.

Better Market Timing

Suitable storage can give farmers and businesses more flexibility when planning the marketing of their products.

Support Food Security

Reducing post-harvest losses can contribute to stronger food availability and agricultural resilience.

Increase Economic Value

Proper storage can help protect the value of agricultural products before they are sold or processed.

Good Storage Practices

1. Keep Storage Areas Clean

Storage facilities should be kept clean and properly maintained to reduce contamination and pest problems.

2. Control Moisture

Appropriate moisture management is important for many agricultural commodities, especially grains and other dry products.

3. Protect Against Pests

Appropriate pest-management practices should be used to protect stored agricultural products.

4. Use Suitable Packaging

Agricultural products should be stored in appropriate packaging suitable for the commodity and storage conditions.

5. Inspect Stored Products

Regular inspection can help identify problems early and reduce potential losses.

Important Notice: Storage requirements vary according to the type of agricultural commodity. Farmers and businesses should follow appropriate technical guidance for each product and facility.

Reducing Post-Harvest Losses

Better storage and warehousing systems can help protect farmers' products, strengthen agricultural value chains and contribute to food security and economic development across Kogi State.
