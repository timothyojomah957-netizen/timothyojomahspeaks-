A Message of Hope, Unity and Agricultural Transformation

A MESSAGE TO THE GOOD PEOPLE OF KOGI STATE

From the Honourable Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security

My dear people of Kogi State,

It is with great humility and a deep sense of responsibility that I extend my warm greetings and heartfelt appreciation to the good people of our dear state.

I sincerely acknowledge and appreciate the visionary leadership of His Excellency, Alhaji Ahmed Usman Ododo, Executive Governor of Kogi State , whose commitment to the development, prosperity, and wellbeing of our people continues to inspire our collective efforts.

I also recognize the invaluable contributions of His Excellency, the Deputy Governor, the Speaker and Honourable Members of the Kogi State House of Assembly, members of the State Executive Council, traditional rulers, religious leaders, community leaders, farmers' associations, development partners, and all other distinguished stakeholders working for the progress of our state.

As Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security , I remain firmly committed to supporting the agricultural transformation agenda of this administration.

Agriculture is not merely an economic activity; it is the foundation of food security, employment, wealth creation, rural development, and a prosperous future for our children.

Our farmers are the backbone of Kogi State. From the rice fields and cassava farms to our yam, maize, soybean, oil palm, fisheries, and livestock sectors, the hardworking men and women across our communities are contributing immensely to the growth of our state.

We will continue to pursue initiatives that empower our farmers, encourage our youths and women to participate meaningfully in agriculture, improve access to agricultural opportunities, strengthen food production, promote modern farming practices, and create stronger connections between farmers and markets.

I therefore call on all citizens to support the administration of His Excellency, Governor Ahmed Usman Ododo , and to remain united in our shared determination to build a stronger and more prosperous Kogi State.

Let us work together.
Let us invest in agriculture.
Let us support our farmers.
Let us protect our communities.
And above all, let us build a Kogi State where food security, opportunity, peace, and prosperity are available to all.

The journey ahead may be challenging, but with unity, determination, innovation, and the continued support of our people, we will succeed.

May God Almighty continue to bless our farmers, our communities, our traditional institutions, our leaders, and every citizen of Kogi State.

Together, we can feed Kogi.
Together, we can grow Kogi.
Together, we can build the Kogi we desire.

Special Acknowledgement

I also wish to sincerely acknowledge and appreciate the entire Ibaji TV Channel team for their dedication, commitment, and continued efforts toward informing, connecting, and promoting the people of Ibaji and Kogi State.

Special recognition goes to the CEO and developer of this website, Inekwe Theophilus Akoji , for his vision, creativity, technical expertise, and dedication invested in developing this digital platform.

This platform represents an important step toward using technology to promote agriculture, share credible information, showcase agricultural opportunities, and connect farmers, government, businesses, and the wider public.

On behalf of the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security, I commend the entire Ibaji TV Channel team and Inekwe Theophilus Akoji for this commendable initiative. May this platform continue to grow and serve the people of Kogi State.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah
Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security
Kogi State Government
