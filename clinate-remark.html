Climate-Smart Agriculture

Promoting agricultural practices that help farmers improve productivity, adapt to changing climate conditions and manage natural resources sustainably.

What Is Climate-Smart Agriculture?

Climate-smart agriculture focuses on practical approaches that can support agricultural productivity, strengthen resilience and promote sustainable management of land, water and other natural resources.

🌱

Resilient Farming

Encouraging farming practices that can help farmers respond to changing environmental and climatic conditions.

💧

Water Management

Promoting efficient and responsible use of water resources for agricultural production.

🌳

Natural Resources

Supporting responsible management of soil, vegetation, water and other resources needed for agriculture.

🌾

Sustainable Production

Encouraging production methods that maintain agricultural productivity while protecting resources.

🚜

Farm Innovation

Promoting appropriate technologies and innovative approaches that can improve farm efficiency.

🌍

Environmental Awareness

Helping agricultural communities understand the importance of protecting the environment while producing food.

Climate-Smart Practices

Soil Conservation

Practices that help protect soil quality and reduce degradation.

Water Conservation

Efficient approaches to managing and conserving water used in agricultural production.

Crop Diversification

Using suitable combinations of crops can help farmers manage production risks and improve resilience.

Agroforestry

Integrating trees and agricultural activities can support soil, environmental and farm management goals.

Improved Farm Management

Better planning and management can help farmers use available resources more efficiently.

Weather Awareness

Access to reliable weather information can help farmers make better decisions about agricultural activities.

Supporting Climate-Smart Agriculture

1. Understand Local Conditions

Farmers should consider local soil, water, weather and production conditions when selecting agricultural practices.

2. Select Suitable Practices

Appropriate farming practices should be selected based on the farmer's production system and available resources.

3. Improve Resource Management

Farmers can improve the efficient use of water, soil, inputs and other agricultural resources.

4. Access Extension Support

Agricultural extension services can help farmers learn about improved practices and technologies.

5. Monitor Results

Farmers can assess the results of adopted practices and make improvements where necessary.

Benefits

Improved Resilience

Climate-smart approaches can help agricultural systems better respond to environmental challenges.

Resource Efficiency

Better management of water, soil and agricultural inputs can contribute to more efficient production.

Sustainable Agriculture

Responsible agricultural practices can support long-term production and environmental sustainability.

Food Security

More resilient agricultural systems can contribute to stable food production and food security.

Building a Resilient Agricultural Future

Farmers, communities, government institutions, researchers and development partners all have a role to play in promoting sustainable and climate-smart agriculture.
