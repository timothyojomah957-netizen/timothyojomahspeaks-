Where Farmers Can Sell Their Produce

Helping farmers identify suitable channels for selling crops, livestock, fish and other agricultural products while promoting responsible and transparent agricultural trade.

Where Can Farmers Sell?

Farmers can explore different legitimate sales channels depending on the type, quantity, quality and location of their agricultural produce.

🏪

Local Markets

Farmers can sell agricultural produce directly to consumers and traders through local markets in their communities.

🤝

Farmer Cooperatives

Cooperatives can help members coordinate production, marketing and connections with potential buyers.

🏭

Processors

Farmers may supply suitable agricultural products to food processors, agro-processing businesses and other value-addition enterprises.

🚚

Wholesalers

Wholesalers may purchase agricultural commodities in larger quantities for distribution to other markets.

🛒

Retailers

Retail businesses may purchase farm products for resale directly to consumers.

🏢

Institutional Buyers

Eligible institutions and organizations may purchase agricultural products according to their procurement requirements.

📦

Aggregators

Agricultural aggregators may collect produce from several farmers and connect supplies with larger markets.

📱

Verified Online Channels

Farmers can explore legitimate digital platforms and online business networks for agricultural marketing.

Market Areas in Kogi State

Farmers should confirm current market activities, opening arrangements, commodity demand and applicable trading requirements before travelling.

Lokoja Area

Farmers around Lokoja can explore local markets, traders, processors, retailers and other agricultural buyers operating in the area.

Okene Area

Farmers in and around Okene can explore appropriate local trading and agricultural distribution channels.

Anyigba / Dekina Area

Farmers in the eastern part of Kogi State can explore agricultural markets, traders, cooperatives and potential commodity buyers.

Idah Area

Farmers can explore local agricultural markets and relevant fisheries, crop and livestock trading opportunities.

Ankpa Area

Farmers can identify appropriate buyers and market channels for crops and other agricultural commodities.

Community Markets

Community markets across Kogi State remain important channels for farmers selling produce directly or through traders.

Tips Before Selling Your Produce

1

Know the Current Market

Check recent market conditions and compare prices from more than one potential buyer where possible.

2

Sort and Grade Your Produce

Separate damaged, poor-quality and good-quality products where appropriate and package them properly.

3

Know Your Quantity

Know the exact quantity available for sale before negotiating with buyers.

4

Compare Buyers

Consider price, transportation costs, payment arrangements and other conditions before accepting an offer.

5

Verify the Buyer

Take reasonable steps to verify the identity and legitimacy of buyers before completing major transactions.

Farmer-to-Buyer Enquiry

Farmers can submit their produce information for market-related enquiries.
