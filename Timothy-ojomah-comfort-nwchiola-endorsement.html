Hon. Timothy Ojomah Endorses Re-Election Bid of Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba

September 2026

The Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Hon. Timothy Ojomah, has publicly endorsed the re-election bid of the Deputy Speaker of the Kogi State House of Assembly and member representing Ibaji Constituency, Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba.

The development was reported by Ibaji TV Channel, which captured the Commissioner's expression of support for the re-election bid of Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba.

The endorsement forms part of the ongoing political activities and engagements surrounding the representation of Ibaji Constituency in the Kogi State House of Assembly. Hon. Timothy Ojomah has publicly made his position known regarding the re-election bid of the Deputy Speaker.

Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba currently serves as Deputy Speaker of the Kogi State House of Assembly while representing Ibaji Constituency. Her re-election bid has consequently become part of the political conversation within the constituency as political activities continue.

According to the report by Ibaji TV Channel, the endorsement provides a public indication of Hon. Timothy Ojomah's support for Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba as the electoral process progresses.

The Commissioner’s endorsement has also drawn attention to the importance of political representation and continued engagement with the people of Ibaji Constituency. Political stakeholders, community leaders, candidates and residents continue to participate in discussions concerning the future representation of the constituency.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah's public statement of support is therefore another development in the political activities surrounding Ibaji Constituency. The endorsement has been documented and reported by Ibaji TV Channel as part of its coverage of political developments in the area.

As the electoral process continues, candidates and political stakeholders are expected to communicate their positions, engage with constituents and present their respective messages to the electorate.

The people of Ibaji Constituency will participate in the electoral process and make their choices among the candidates taking part in the contest.

This report was published by Timothy Ojomah Speaks based on the report and coverage by Ibaji TV Channel.
