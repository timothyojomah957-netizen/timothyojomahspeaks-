How the Connection Works

The enquiry process is designed to make it easier for farmers and agricultural buyers to communicate their needs.

1

Submit Enquiry

Provide your contact information and details about the product or agricultural opportunity.

2

Review Information

The submitted information can be reviewed for appropriate agricultural communication and follow-up.

3

Connect

Where appropriate, farmers and buyers may be connected for further communication.

4

Discuss Terms

Farmers and buyers can discuss product quality, quantity, price, delivery and other relevant terms.

Protect Your Information

Do not enter passwords, ATM PINs, bank PINs or other highly sensitive financial information into this form. Only provide information needed for your agricultural enquiry.
