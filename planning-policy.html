Policy Priorities

Food Security

Supporting policies and programs that increase agricultural production and improve food availability.

Farmer Empowerment

Promoting initiatives that improve opportunities, knowledge, productivity and livelihoods for farmers.

Youth & Women

Encouraging inclusive policies that expand agricultural opportunities for young people and women.

Agribusiness

Supporting policies that encourage investment, processing, marketing and value addition within agriculture.

Innovation

Promoting appropriate technology, research and innovation to improve agricultural productivity and competitiveness.

Sustainability

Encouraging responsible agricultural practices that support long-term environmental and economic sustainability.

Planning & Policy Process

1. Identify Priorities

Identify major agricultural needs, challenges and opportunities that require policy attention.

2. Gather Evidence

Use agricultural data, research, consultations and other relevant information to support planning decisions.

3. Consult Stakeholders

Engage farmers, communities, businesses, experts and relevant institutions in agricultural planning.

4. Develop Strategies

Develop practical strategies, programs and initiatives that respond to identified agricultural priorities.

5. Monitor Results

Review implementation and results to identify lessons and improve future agricultural policies and programs.

Planning for a Stronger Agricultural Future

Effective agricultural planning and sound policy development provide a foundation for sustainable agricultural growth, stronger livelihoods and improved food security across Kogi State.
