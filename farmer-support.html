Farmer Support

Providing farmers with information, guidance and access to relevant agricultural support opportunities across Kogi State.

How We Support Farmers

Farmer support involves connecting farmers with useful agricultural information, training, extension services, programmes and other opportunities that can contribute to improved agricultural activities.

🌱

Crop Support

Information and guidance relating to crop production, farm management, planting, harvesting and responsible agricultural practices.

🐄

Livestock Support

Information on livestock production, animal management and appropriate husbandry practices.

🎓

Training

Information about agricultural training, workshops, demonstrations and farmer capacity-building activities.

🚜

Mechanization

Information about appropriate agricultural machinery, equipment and modern farming technologies.

💰

Funding Information

Information about relevant agricultural financing and funding opportunities, subject to programme requirements.

🛒

Market Support

Information that can help farmers understand buyers, markets, agricultural value chains and marketing opportunities.

Available Support Areas

Agricultural Extension Services

Farmers can seek appropriate agricultural information and technical guidance through relevant extension services.

Farmer Registration

Farmers can register their agricultural information to help connect them with relevant programmes and agricultural information.

Agricultural Education

Farmers can access useful educational information about modern farming practices, farm management and agricultural technologies.

Farmer and Buyer Connection

Farmers and buyers can submit enquiries concerning agricultural products, buying and selling opportunities.

Women and Youth Agriculture

Relevant agricultural opportunities and programmes may support participation by women and young people in agriculture.

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Farmers can learn about agricultural practices that promote responsible management of farm resources and climate-related risks.

How Farmers Can Request Support

1

Identify Your Need

Determine the type of agricultural information, training, funding, market or technical support you require.

2

Register or Submit an Enquiry

Use the farmer registration or enquiry page to provide the information required for your request.

3

Provide Accurate Information

Ensure that your contact and agricultural information is accurate so that relevant communication can reach you.

4

Follow Official Updates

Check official agricultural announcements and programme information for available opportunities and requirements.

Important Notice

Registration or submission of an enquiry does not automatically mean that a farmer qualifies for a particular programme or financial opportunity. Eligibility is subject to the requirements of the relevant programme.

Need Farmer Support?

Farmers can use the available registration and enquiry channels to communicate their agricultural needs and seek relevant information.
