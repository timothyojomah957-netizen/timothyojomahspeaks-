The Agricultural Heritage of Odeke

Odeke is a community in Ibaji Local Government Area of Kogi State with a strong connection to agriculture. Farming has traditionally played an important role in the livelihood and development of families and communities in the area.

The agricultural heritage of Odeke represents the knowledge, traditions and hard work passed from one generation of farmers to another. The land provides opportunities for crop farming, livestock production, fishing and other agricultural activities.

Today, the agricultural heritage of Odeke continues to provide a foundation for promoting food production, youth participation, women's empowerment and sustainable rural development.

Preserving Our Agricultural Heritage

The agricultural heritage of Odeke should be preserved while embracing modern agricultural practices. By supporting farmers with knowledge, technology, financing, improved inputs and access to markets, the agricultural potential of Odeke and Ibaji can continue to grow.
