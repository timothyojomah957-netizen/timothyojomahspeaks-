Fisheries & Aquaculture

Promoting sustainable fish production, aquaculture development, employment, food security and economic opportunities for fish farmers across Kogi State.

Fisheries and Aquaculture Development

Fisheries and aquaculture can contribute to food security, nutrition, employment and income generation while creating opportunities across production, processing and marketing.

Supporting the Fisheries Value Chain

The Ministry encourages responsible agricultural development across different value chains. Fisheries and aquaculture provide opportunities for farmers and entrepreneurs to participate in fish production, processing, storage and marketing.


The development of practical skills, improved production methods, responsible water-resource management and access to markets can help strengthen the fisheries sector.

Responsible Fish Farming

Sustainable aquaculture requires careful planning, proper management and responsible use of resources.

1

Select a Suitable Location

Farmers should assess the suitability of the proposed location, water availability and other important production requirements.

2

Prepare the Production System

Ponds, tanks or other approved production systems should be properly prepared and managed.

3

Manage Fish Health

Farmers should maintain appropriate water quality, feeding and general fish health management practices.

4

Keep Farm Records

Production, feeding, expenses and sales records can help farmers monitor their businesses and make informed decisions.

5

Plan for the Market

Farmers should consider market demand, buyers, processing and distribution before harvesting.

Important Information

Farmers and prospective fish farmers should seek appropriate technical guidance and verify official government programmes, training opportunities and support initiatives through authorized Ministry channels.
