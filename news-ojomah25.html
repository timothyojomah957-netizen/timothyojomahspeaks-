Ojomah Family Marks 25th Anniversary of Late Chief Peter Obodo-Eze Ojomah With Thanksgiving

Odeke, Ibaji Local Government Area, Kogi State

IBAJI TV NEWS INTRODUCTION

FROM THE HEART OF ODEKE IN IBAJI LOCAL GOVERNMENT AREA OF KOGI STATE, A MOMENT OF REMEMBRANCE, GRATITUDE AND FAMILY LEGACY HAS TAKEN CENTRE STAGE.

TWENTY-FIVE YEARS AFTER THE PASSING OF THEIR BELOVED PATRIARCH, LATE CHIEF PETER OBODO-EZE OJOMAH, THE OJOMAH FAMILY HAS COME TOGETHER TO HONOUR HIS MEMORY, CELEBRATE HIS LIFE AND REFLECT ON A LEGACY THAT CONTINUES TO RESONATE ACROSS GENERATIONS.

THE ANNIVERSARY, MARKED WITH THANKSGIVING AND PRAYERS, BROUGHT TOGETHER FAMILY MEMBERS, RELATIVES, FRIENDS, ASSOCIATES AND WELL-WISHERS, AS THEY PAID TRIBUTE TO A MAN REMEMBERED FOR HIS WISDOM, INTEGRITY, HUMILITY, LOVE FOR FAMILY AND COMMITMENT TO HIS COMMUNITY.

IBAJI TV WAS THERE TO CAPTURE THE MOMENTS, THE MEMORIES AND THE MESSAGE OF A FAMILY DETERMINED TO KEEP THE LEGACY OF THEIR PATRIARCH ALIVE.

HERE IS OUR REPORT.

A QUARTER-CENTURY OF MEMORY AND LEGACY

ODEKE, IBAJI — The Ojomah Family of Odeke in Ibaji Local Government Area of Kogi State has marked the 25th anniversary of the passing of its beloved patriarch, Late Chief Peter Obodo-Eze Ojomah, with a solemn thanksgiving and remembrance.

The occasion brought together members of the Ojomah family, relatives, friends, associates and well-wishers who gathered to honour the memory of a man whose life was defined by wisdom, integrity, humility, love for family and commitment to his community.

Twenty-five years may have passed since his departure from this world, but for the Ojomah family, the passage of time has neither diminished his memory nor erased the impact of his life.

A LIFE THAT CONTINUES TO INSPIRE

The thanksgiving served as an opportunity for the family to reflect on the life of Late Chief Peter Obodo-Eze Ojomah and the enduring values he left behind.

The family expressed gratitude to Almighty God for the gift of his life and for the legacy he established during his lifetime.

Beyond his immediate family, he is remembered as a man who cherished his community and maintained a strong sense of responsibility towards those around him.

For the Ojomah family, his legacy is not simply a memory of the past, but a continuing source of inspiration for the present and future generations.

FAMILY LEGACY AND PUBLIC SERVICE

The occasion also highlighted the continuing presence and contributions of members of the Ojomah family to the development of their people.

Particular appreciation was expressed for the presence and leadership of Hon. Timothy Ojomah, Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Kogi State.

The family noted that his dedication to public service and commitment to the development of his people reflect values that remain strongly associated with the Ojomah family heritage.

His presence at the anniversary further underscored the importance attached by the family to preserving the values, principles and traditions passed down by their late patriarch.

GRATITUDE TO FAMILY, FRIENDS AND WELL-WISHERS

As family members gathered in thanksgiving and remembrance, the Ojomah Family also expressed sincere appreciation to their relatives, friends, associates and well-wishers who joined them for the occasion.

The family acknowledged the prayers, goodwill, support and presence of those who stood with them in remembering a man whose life continues to hold a special place in their hearts.

For those gathered in Odeke, the anniversary was both solemn and celebratory — solemn because it marked the passing of a beloved patriarch, and celebratory because it provided an opportunity to appreciate the life he lived and the legacy he left behind.

TWENTY-FIVE YEARS GONE, BUT NEVER FORGOTTEN

Twenty-five years after his passing, the name of Late Chief Peter Obodo-Eze Ojomah continues to remain deeply rooted in the memories of those who knew and loved him.

His children, grandchildren, relatives and loved ones continue to carry forward the values associated with his life, ensuring that his story remains part of the family's history.

The Ojomah Family prayed that Almighty God would continue to grant eternal rest to the soul of their late patriarch, preserve the family he left behind and guide future generations to uphold the values for which he was remembered.

TWENTY-FIVE YEARS GONE, BUT NEVER FORGOTTEN.

A LIFE REMEMBERED.
A LEGACY PRESERVED.
A NAME THAT LIVES ON.
