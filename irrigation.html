Agricultural Irrigation

Promoting responsible water management and suitable irrigation practices to support agricultural production and improve farming opportunities.

Understanding Agricultural Irrigation

Irrigation involves supplying water to agricultural land when rainfall alone is not sufficient. Appropriate irrigation can support crop production, especially when water resources are properly managed.

💧

Water Management

Good water management helps farmers use available water resources efficiently and responsibly.

🌱

Crop Production

Suitable irrigation can provide crops with water required for healthy growth when rainfall is insufficient.

🚜

Farm Development

Reliable water management can support farmers in planning and developing appropriate agricultural activities.

🌾

Dry Season Farming

Where conditions are suitable, irrigation can support agricultural activities during periods of limited rainfall.

📈

Productivity

Properly managed irrigation may contribute to improved agricultural productivity and more consistent production.

🌍

Sustainable Water Use

Responsible irrigation practices help protect water resources, farmland and the wider environment.

Irrigation Methods

Drip Irrigation

Drip systems deliver water close to plant roots and can help reduce unnecessary water loss when properly designed and maintained.

Sprinkler Irrigation

Sprinkler systems distribute water over crops and may be suitable for certain farming conditions and production systems.

Surface Irrigation

Surface irrigation uses the movement of water across agricultural land and should be properly managed to reduce wastage and erosion.

Water Storage

Appropriate water storage facilities can help farmers manage water availability for suitable agricultural activities.

Important Notice: Farmers should assess local water availability, soil conditions, environmental considerations and crop requirements before establishing an irrigation system. Appropriate technical advice should be obtained where necessary.
