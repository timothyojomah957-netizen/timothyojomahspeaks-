About Ibaji

Ibaji is a Local Government Area of Kogi State with a strong connection to agriculture and rural livelihoods. The communities are blessed with land and natural resources that provide opportunities for crop production, livestock farming, fishing and other agricultural activities.

Agriculture remains an important source of livelihood for many families in Ibaji. The hardworking farmers of the area contribute to food production while creating opportunities for economic development and rural empowerment.

Our Agricultural Vision for Ibaji

The vision is to promote modern and sustainable agriculture, improve farmers' access to agricultural inputs and financing, create better markets for farm produce and encourage young people and women to participate actively in agriculture.

By strengthening farmers and connecting them with markets, technology, financing and government agricultural programmes, Ibaji can further develop its agricultural potential and contribute significantly to food security in Kogi State.
