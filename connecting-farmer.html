Connecting Farmers with Agricultural Programmes

Helping farmers access relevant agricultural programmes, information, training opportunities, extension support and other initiatives that can strengthen farming and rural livelihoods.

Connecting Farmers to Opportunities

Agricultural programmes can provide farmers with access to knowledge, technical support, inputs, training, markets and other forms of assistance.

Making Agricultural Support More Accessible

The Ministry works to improve communication between farmers and agricultural programmes by providing information about relevant opportunities and directing farmers toward appropriate channels for participation.


Programme eligibility, registration requirements, locations and availability may vary. Farmers should always confirm current programme information through authorized Ministry channels before submitting personal information or paying any fees.

How Farmers Can Connect

1

Get Programme Information

Farmers should check official Ministry announcements and authorized agricultural information channels for available programmes.

2

Check Eligibility

Read the programme requirements carefully to determine whether the programme is suitable for your farm or agricultural enterprise.

3

Register Through the Right Channel

Complete registration through the official programme process and provide only information that is legitimately required.

4

Attend Required Training

Where training is part of a programme, farmers should participate actively and follow the relevant technical guidance.

5

Maintain Contact

Farmers should keep appropriate records and contact programme officers or extension personnel when follow-up support is required.

🤝 Partnerships

📢 Announcements

Information about programme opportunities, meetings and agricultural activities.

👨‍🌾 Farmer Groups

Support for farmers working through cooperatives and organized groups.

Important Farmer Notice

Always verify agricultural programme information through official sources. Farmers should be cautious of individuals or websites asking for money or sensitive personal information in exchange for programme registration or guaranteed benefits.
