Service Projects & Initiatives

A collection of agricultural and community development initiatives focused on improving livelihoods, supporting farmers and promoting food security.

1. Support for Farmers

Initiatives aimed at supporting farmers and improving agricultural productivity through access to opportunities, knowledge and agricultural resources.

2. Food Security

Programmes designed to encourage increased food production and strengthen food security across communities.

3. Agricultural Development

Promotion of modern agricultural practices and sustainable farming approaches that can improve productivity and create economic opportunities.

4. Youth in Agriculture

Encouraging young people to participate in agriculture and explore farming and agribusiness as opportunities for employment and entrepreneurship.

5. Community Development

Engagement with communities and stakeholders to identify agricultural needs and support initiatives that contribute to local development.

6. Sustainable Agriculture

Promoting responsible agricultural practices that protect the environment while supporting farmers and ensuring long-term food production.
