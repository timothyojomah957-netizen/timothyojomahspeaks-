Hon. Timothy Ojomah Urges Ibaji Political Appointees to Intensify Grassroots Mobilisation for APC Victory

Published: August 26, 2026

Hon. Timothy Ojomah, the Kogi State Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, has called on all political appointees from Ibaji Local Government Area to increase their commitment, strengthen grassroots mobilisation and work collectively towards the victory of the All Progressives Congress (APC) in the forthcoming general elections.

The Commissioner made the call while emphasizing the important role political appointees play in connecting the government and the party with the people at the grassroots. He urged all appointees from Ibaji to return to their various wards, communities and constituencies, engage directly with the people and ensure that the achievements and programmes of the APC-led administration are properly communicated to the electorate.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah stressed that electoral success cannot be achieved through individual efforts alone, but requires unity, teamwork, dedication and continuous engagement with the people. He therefore encouraged all political appointees and APC stakeholders in Ibaji to put aside differences, work together and build a stronger political structure across the local government.

He further urged the appointees to listen to the concerns, needs and aspirations of the people and maintain a strong relationship with their communities. According to him, effective grassroots mobilisation begins with being present among the people, understanding their challenges and working together to promote the ideals and programmes of the APC.

The Commissioner also called for greater cooperation among party leaders, political appointees, ward executives, youth leaders, women leaders and other stakeholders across Ibaji. He emphasized that every member of the party has an important role to play in strengthening the APC's presence and ensuring that the party remains united and organised ahead of the elections.

He noted that Ibaji remains an important political constituency, and the commitment of its political stakeholders will be critical to achieving a strong electoral performance for the APC. He therefore encouraged everyone involved to remain focused, disciplined and committed to the collective political objective.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah urged the political appointees not to relent in their efforts, but to intensify consultations, community engagement and mobilisation across the various wards of Ibaji Local Government Area.

He called on APC members and supporters to remain united, avoid unnecessary divisions and work together in the spirit of progress and collective responsibility.

As the forthcoming elections draw closer, the Commissioner encouraged the political stakeholders to remain committed to grassroots engagement and continue working with the people across Ibaji Local Government Area.

The call is expected to further strengthen political mobilisation and coordination among APC stakeholders in Ibaji as preparations for the forthcoming elections continue.
