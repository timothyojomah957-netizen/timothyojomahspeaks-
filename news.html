KOGI STATE @35: COMMISSIONER FOR AGRICULTURE AND FOOD SECURITY CELEBRATES 35 YEARS OF UNITY, RESILIENCE AND PROGRESS

📅 Published: August 27, 2026

As Kogi State commemorates the 35th anniversary of its creation, the Honourable Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security extends warm congratulations to the Government and the good people of Kogi State on this historic and significant milestone.

The 35th anniversary provides an opportunity to reflect on Kogi State's journey, celebrate the achievements recorded over the years and recognise the resilience, unity and determination of its people.

The Commissioner celebrates the people of Kogi State for their continued contributions to the growth and development of the state. He also acknowledges farmers, agricultural workers, entrepreneurs, civil servants, traditional leaders, youths, women and all citizens who continue to contribute their quota to the progress of the state.

🌾 Building a stronger Kogi through agriculture, food security, farmer empowerment and sustainable economic development.

As the state moves forward, the Commissioner reaffirmed the importance of agriculture as a major pillar of economic development and food security. He expressed optimism that continued cooperation between government, farmers, stakeholders and communities will help create greater opportunities and improve the livelihoods of the people.

On this special occasion, the Commissioner wishes the Government and people of Kogi State a joyful 35th anniversary celebration and calls for continued unity, peace, cooperation and commitment to the development of the state.

Happy 35th Anniversary to Kogi State! May Kogi State continue to grow, prosper and remain a shining example of resilience and progress.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah Urges Ibaji Political Appointees to Intensify Grassroots Mobilisation for APC Victory

Published: August 26, 2026

Hon. Timothy Ojomah, the Kogi State Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, has called on all political appointees from Ibaji Local Government Area to increase their commitment, strengthen grassroots mobilisation and work collectively towards the victory of the All Progressives Congress (APC) in the forthcoming general elections.

The Commissioner emphasized the important role political appointees play in connecting the government and the party with the people at the grassroots.

He urged all appointees from Ibaji to return to their various wards, communities and constituencies, engage directly with the people and communicate government programmes effectively.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah stressed that electoral success requires unity, teamwork, dedication and continuous engagement with the people.

He encouraged APC stakeholders in Ibaji to put aside differences, work together and build a stronger political structure across the local government.

He further urged the appointees to listen to the concerns, needs and aspirations of the people and maintain strong relationships with their communities.

The Commissioner also called for greater cooperation among party leaders, political appointees, ward executives, youth leaders, women leaders and other stakeholders across Ibaji.

He noted that Ibaji remains an important political constituency and encouraged all stakeholders to remain focused, disciplined and committed.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah urged political appointees not to relent in their efforts but to intensify consultations, community engagement and mobilisation across the various wards.
