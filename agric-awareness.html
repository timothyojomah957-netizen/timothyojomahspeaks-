Agricultural Awareness

Promoting agricultural knowledge, public awareness and timely information for farmers, communities and other stakeholders across Kogi State.

Awareness Areas

Agricultural awareness helps farmers and communities access useful information and understand opportunities, programmes and responsible agricultural practices.

🌱

Farming Awareness

Sharing useful information about responsible farming practices, production methods and farm management.

🌾

Food Security

Promoting awareness about food production, food security and the importance of sustainable agriculture.

🐄

Livestock Awareness

Providing information that can help livestock farmers improve animal management and farm practices.

🌍

Climate Awareness

Raising awareness about climate-related agricultural challenges and practical approaches to resilience.

📢

Programme Information

Communicating relevant agricultural programmes, initiatives, opportunities and official announcements.

👨‍🌾

Farmer Education

Helping farmers access simple and useful agricultural information through appropriate communication channels.

Awareness Channels

Agricultural information can be communicated through different channels to reach farmers and communities effectively.

Radio

Agricultural programmes and discussions can help reach farming communities.

Television

Television can support public education and agricultural information campaigns.

Community Outreach

Direct community engagement can help communicate information at the local level.

Digital Platforms

Websites and social media can provide timely agricultural information to a wider audience.

Workshops

Meetings, seminars and workshops can provide opportunities for direct learning and discussion.

Extension Activities

Agricultural extension activities can connect farmers with practical information and technical guidance.

Public Awareness Process

1

Identify Information Needs

Determine the information farmers and communities need most.

2

Prepare Clear Information

Develop simple, accurate and relevant agricultural information for the intended audience.

3

Reach the Community

Use suitable communication channels to share information with farmers and other stakeholders.

4

Encourage Feedback

Listen to farmers and communities to understand their questions, concerns and information needs.

Official Information

For official agricultural announcements, programmes and opportunities, visitors should rely on information published or confirmed through authorized Ministry channels.

Stay Informed

Follow official Ministry communication channels for agricultural news, announcements, programmes and public awareness activities.
