Who Can Apply?

Eligibility requirements may differ depending on the agricultural programme, loan, grant or support opportunity. Farmers should carefully review the requirements of each specific programme before applying.

Basic Eligibility Requirements

  • Applicant should be genuinely involved or interested in eligible agricultural activities.
  • Applicant should provide accurate personal information.
  • Applicant should provide valid contact information.
  • Applicant may be required to provide identification documents.
  • Applicant may need to provide information about their farm, business or agricultural activity.
  • Applicant must meet the requirements of the specific programme.
  • Applicant should be willing to comply with applicable programme rules and conditions.

Farming Information That May Be Required

🌾 Farm Type

Information about the type of farming activity, such as crop farming, livestock, poultry or fisheries.

📐 Farm Size

Applicants may be asked to provide information about the size or scale of their farming operation.

📍 Farm Location

Details about the farm location, community and Local Government Area may be required.

📊 Production Details

Applicants may provide information about crops, livestock, fish production or other agricultural activities.

Possible Documents Required

Depending on the programme, applicants may be asked to provide some of the following documents or information.

  • Valid identification document.
  • Phone number and contact information.
  • Proof of residence where applicable.
  • Farm or agricultural business information.
  • Cooperative or farmer-group information where applicable.
  • Business registration documents where required.
  • Other documents specified by the particular programme.

Special Programme Categories

Important Information

Meeting the general requirements listed on this page does not automatically guarantee approval for a loan, grant or other agricultural support. Each programme may have its own eligibility conditions, selection process, funding limits and documentation requirements.
