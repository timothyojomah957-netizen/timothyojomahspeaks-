HON. TIMOTHY OJOMAH CONGRATULATES HON. ALEX UKWELA ON HIS EMERGENCE AS IBAJI LGA CHAIRMAN-ELECT

By IBAJI TV Channel | 18 October 2026

Hon. Timothy Ojomah congratulates Hon. Alex Ukwela on his emergence as Chairman-Elect of Ibaji Local Government Area, Kogi State.

The emergence of a new local government chairman marks an important moment for grassroots leadership and community development. It also provides an opportunity to renew the commitment to peace, unity, cooperation and service to the people.

Ibaji Local Government Area is home to communities whose progress depends on collaboration between residents, community leaders and public office holders. Constructive engagement and a shared commitment to development can help communities address their needs and work towards a better future.

Effective local government leadership involves listening to the people, promoting accountability and supporting initiatives that improve the wellbeing of residents. Peaceful coexistence and respect for different views are also important in building a united community.

Agriculture and food security remain significant to the livelihoods of many families in Kogi State. Continued attention to rural development, food production, infrastructure and economic opportunities can contribute to the long-term progress of communities.

As Ibaji looks towards the next chapter of local government administration, residents will be looking for leadership that responds to community needs and promotes inclusive development.

The message of congratulations highlights the importance of unity, responsible leadership and a collective commitment to the advancement of Ibaji Local Government Area.
