HON. TIMOTHY OJOMAH CONGRATULATES HON. ALEX UKWELA, THANKS GOVERNOR ODODO FOR PEACEFUL COUNCIL POLLS

Published by Timothy Ojomah Speaks | October 18, 2026
A handshake symbolising mutual respect and the importance of cooperation in leadership.

The Honourable Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Hon. Timothy Ojomah, has congratulated the Chairman-elect of Ibaji Local Government Area, Hon. Alex Ukwela, on his emergence, describing the occasion as an opportunity to strengthen grassroots governance, promote unity, and advance development across the local government.

Hon. Ojomah extended his warm congratulations to Hon. Ukwela, expressing hope that his emergence would usher in a new chapter of purposeful leadership, peace, unity, and progress for the people of Ibaji. He noted that the responsibility of leading a local government goes beyond occupying an office; it requires dedication, accountability, inclusiveness, and a genuine commitment to improving the lives of the people.

OJOMAH COMMENDS GOVERNOR ODODO FOR PEACEFUL COUNCIL POLLS

The Commissioner also expressed his appreciation to the Executive Governor of Kogi State, His Excellency, Alhaji Ahmed Usman Ododo, for the peaceful conduct of the council elections. He commended the Governor for his leadership and commitment to an atmosphere that allowed the electoral process to take place peacefully.

Hon. Ojomah noted that peaceful elections are essential to strengthening democratic institutions, promoting political stability, and ensuring that citizens can participate in choosing leaders at the grassroots level. He emphasized the importance of sustaining the spirit of peace, understanding, and cooperation among political stakeholders and the people of Kogi State.

He further acknowledged the importance of collective responsibility in consolidating democracy and called on political leaders, supporters, and residents to continue embracing peace and mutual respect, irrespective of political differences.

A CALL FOR PURPOSEFUL LEADERSHIP IN IBAJI

Speaking on the responsibility ahead, the Commissioner urged Hon. Alex Ukwela to pilot the affairs of Ibaji Local Government with wisdom, humility, fairness, and a strong sense of responsibility. He emphasized the need for the Chairman-elect to place the interests of the people above personal considerations and ensure that the dividends of democracy reach every community within the local government.

According to Hon. Ojomah, the people of Ibaji deserve leadership that listens to their concerns, understands their needs, and works tirelessly to address the challenges confronting the area. He encouraged the incoming administration to embrace consultation and collaboration with community leaders, traditional rulers, youths, women, and other relevant stakeholders in building a shared vision for the development of Ibaji.

PRIORITISING GRASSROOTS DEVELOPMENT

The Commissioner also stressed the need to prioritise critical areas such as rural development, agriculture, youth empowerment, security, education, healthcare, and the provision of essential social amenities. He maintained that sustainable development can be achieved through people-oriented leadership and practical solutions to the challenges facing the grassroots.

Furthermore, Hon. Ojomah encouraged Hon. Ukwela to promote peace and unity among the people, noting that political differences should never become obstacles to collective progress. He called on all stakeholders and residents of Ibaji Local Government to support the incoming administration and contribute positively to building a stronger, more peaceful, and prosperous local government.

ACCOUNTABILITY AND SERVICE TO THE PEOPLE

Hon. Ojomah emphasised that effective leadership requires transparency, accountability, patience, and the courage to make decisions that serve the common good. He therefore urged the Chairman-elect to see his emergence as a call to service and an opportunity to leave a lasting legacy through responsible governance and meaningful development initiatives.

He expressed optimism that, through cooperation, mutual understanding, and a shared commitment to progress, Ibaji Local Government would continue to advance and create better opportunities for its people.

The Commissioner concluded by wishing Hon. Alex Ukwela a successful tenure, urging him to lead with the fear of God, remain accessible to the people, and ensure that the affairs of Ibaji Local Government are managed in a manner that reflects the hopes and aspirations of its citizens.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah congratulates Hon. Alex Ukwela on his emergence as Chairman-elect of Ibaji Local Government Area and expresses appreciation to His Excellency, Alhaji Ahmed Usman Ododo, the Executive Governor of Kogi State, for the peaceful conduct of the council polls.

Congratulations to Hon. Alex Ukwela! May your tenure bring peace, unity, progress, and meaningful development to the people of Ibaji Local Government Area.
