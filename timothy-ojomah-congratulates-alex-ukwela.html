Hon. Timothy Ojomah Congratulates Hon. Alex Ukwela on His Emergence as Ibaji LGA Chairman-Elect

The Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security calls for responsible leadership, unity and sustainable development in Ibaji Local Government Area.

Published: October 18, 2026 | Timothy Ojomah Speaks

Hon. Timothy Ojomah and Hon. Alex Ukwela.

The Honourable Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security in Kogi State, Hon. Timothy Ojomah, has congratulated Hon. Alex Ukwela on his emergence as Chairman-elect of Ibaji Local Government Area, describing the development as an important opportunity to strengthen grassroots leadership and promote meaningful development across the local government.

Hon. Ojomah extended his goodwill message to the Chairman-elect, expressing confidence that the new leadership can make a positive contribution to the welfare of the people through responsible governance, inclusiveness and a strong commitment to public service.

A Call for Responsible Leadership

While congratulating Hon. Ukwela, the Commissioner urged him to approach the responsibility entrusted to him with humility, dedication, fairness and a clear vision for the future of Ibaji Local Government Area.

He emphasized the importance of listening to the people, respecting their concerns and ensuring that the interests of the entire local government remain at the centre of government decisions. According to him, leadership should be measured not only by promises but also by the positive impact it makes on the lives of ordinary citizens.

Hon. Ojomah encouraged the Chairman-elect to promote peace, unity, accountability and cooperation among community leaders, traditional institutions, youths, women and other stakeholders.

The people of Ibaji deserve leadership that listens, serves with integrity, promotes unity and works consistently towards the common good.

Prioritising the Development of Ibaji

The Commissioner stressed the need for the incoming administration to focus on the practical needs of the people, including improved access to basic amenities, support for local economic activities, opportunities for young people and initiatives that can improve residents' livelihoods.

He encouraged the Chairman-elect to work closely with relevant government institutions and community stakeholders to identify local priorities and pursue sustainable solutions to the challenges facing the people.

Commendation for Governor Ahmed Usman Ododo

Hon. Timothy Ojomah also expressed appreciation to the Executive Governor of Kogi State, His Excellency Alhaji Ahmed Usman Ododo, for his leadership and commitment to maintaining peace and stability in the state during the local government election process.

He commended the peaceful atmosphere surrounding the council polls and noted that a peaceful electoral process is important for strengthening democratic participation and allowing citizens to exercise their civic responsibilities.

The Commissioner emphasized that peace, political tolerance and respect for democratic institutions are essential to the progress of Kogi State. He encouraged political leaders and residents to continue supporting an atmosphere of unity and cooperation beyond the election period.

A Message of Hope for Ibaji

Concluding his message, Hon. Ojomah wished Hon. Alex Ukwela a successful tenure and urged him to see his emergence as a call to service rather than merely an achievement.

He called on residents and stakeholders in Ibaji Local Government Area to support initiatives that promote peace, development and the collective interest of the community.

The Commissioner reiterated that genuine progress requires cooperation between leaders and the people, adding that a united community is better positioned to overcome challenges and pursue lasting development.

He wished the Chairman-elect wisdom, strength and success as he prepares to take on the responsibilities of leading Ibaji Local Government Area.
