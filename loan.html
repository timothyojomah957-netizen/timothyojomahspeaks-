Agricultural Grants & Funding Support

Information and guidance for farmers, cooperatives, agribusinesses, women and youth seeking legitimate agricultural funding opportunities.

Types of Agricultural Funding

Agricultural funding may come through different programmes and institutions. Applicants should always confirm the official requirements and application conditions for each opportunity.

How to Apply for Agricultural Funding

1

Find an Official Programme

Check official announcements and verified agricultural institutions for available funding opportunities.

2

Check Eligibility

Read the programme requirements carefully to determine whether you qualify.

3

Prepare Your Documents

Prepare the documents requested by the official programme, such as identification, farmer information or business documents where applicable.

4

Submit Your Application

Submit applications only through the official application channel provided by the programme.

5

Keep Your Application Information

Keep copies of submitted documents and any official application reference information for future enquiries.

General Eligibility Considerations

Specific requirements differ from one programme to another. Applicants may be required to provide some of the following:


  • Valid identification and contact information.
  • Farmer, cooperative or business information.
  • Details of the proposed agricultural activity.
  • Information about the farm, enterprise or project.
  • Estimated production or business plan where required.
  • Relevant supporting documents requested by the programme.
  • Compliance with the official programme guidelines.
