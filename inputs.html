Agricultural Inputs

Supporting farmers with information about appropriate agricultural inputs that can contribute to productive, efficient and sustainable farming.

Types of Agricultural Inputs

Agricultural inputs are resources used by farmers to support different stages of agricultural production. Farmers should use appropriate inputs according to recommended practices and available guidance.

🌱

Seeds

Quality seeds suitable for the farming environment can support good crop establishment and productive farming.

🌾

Fertilizers

Appropriate fertilizers can help provide nutrients required for healthy crop growth when properly selected and applied.

🧪

Crop Protection

Crop protection products and practices can help farmers manage agricultural pests, diseases and other production challenges.

🚜

Farm Equipment

Farm tools, machinery and equipment can support land preparation, planting, harvesting and other agricultural activities.

🐄

Livestock Inputs

Livestock production may require appropriate feed, equipment and other resources for responsible animal management.

💧

Water & Irrigation

Appropriate water management and irrigation resources can support agricultural production where suitable.

Responsible Use of Agricultural Inputs

Use Appropriate Inputs

Farmers should select inputs that are suitable for their crops, livestock, farming conditions and production objectives.

Follow Expert Guidance

Farmers should seek advice from qualified agricultural extension personnel and other appropriate professionals before using unfamiliar agricultural inputs.

Buy From Reliable Sources

Farmers should obtain agricultural inputs from legitimate and trustworthy suppliers and verify product information where necessary.

Store Inputs Properly

Agricultural inputs should be stored safely and according to appropriate product instructions.

Keep Farm Records

Keeping records of inputs purchased and used can help farmers monitor farm expenses and production activities.

Important Notice

The availability, distribution and eligibility requirements for any government-supported agricultural inputs may vary by programme. Farmers should confirm announcements through official Ministry channels before taking action.
