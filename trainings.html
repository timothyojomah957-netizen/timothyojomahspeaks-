Agricultural Training & Capacity Building

Building the knowledge, skills and capacity of farmers, young people, women, cooperatives and agricultural stakeholders across Kogi State.

Training Areas

Agricultural training helps farmers and stakeholders improve production, management, processing, marketing and other activities across the agricultural value chain.

🌱

Crop Production

Training on improved farming practices and responsible crop production.

  • Land preparation
  • Planting practices
  • Crop management
  • Harvesting
🐄

Livestock Management

Capacity building for farmers involved in livestock production.

  • Animal management
  • Feeding practices
  • Animal health awareness
  • Farm hygiene
🏭

Processing & Value Addition

Training designed to help stakeholders improve the handling and processing of agricultural products.

  • Processing
  • Packaging
  • Storage
  • Quality improvement
💼

Agribusiness

Practical knowledge for individuals and groups interested in agricultural businesses.

  • Business planning
  • Record keeping
  • Market planning
  • Financial awareness
📱

Agricultural Technology

Promoting awareness of appropriate technologies that can support modern agricultural activities.

  • Farm technology
  • Digital information
  • Modern techniques
  • Information sharing
🌍

Sustainable Agriculture

Encouraging farming approaches that support long-term productivity and responsible resource use.

  • Soil management
  • Resource conservation
  • Environmental awareness
  • Climate-smart practices

Who Can Benefit?

Farmers

Practical knowledge to improve agricultural production.

Youth

Skills and knowledge for participation in agriculture and agribusiness.

Women

Capacity building for women participating in agricultural activities.

Cooperatives

Training that can strengthen group organization and productivity.

Agribusinesses

Knowledge to improve agricultural business activities and value chains.

How Training Can Work

1. Identify Training Needs

Agricultural training should respond to the needs and challenges experienced by farmers and stakeholders.

2. Select Appropriate Topics

Training topics can be selected according to farming activities, agricultural value chains and local requirements.

3. Practical Learning

Practical demonstrations and field-based learning can help participants understand agricultural techniques.

4. Follow-Up Support

Continued communication and agricultural extension support can help participants apply what they have learned.

Training Information

Details of specific training programmes, dates, venues and registration requirements should be confirmed through official Ministry channels.
