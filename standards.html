Good Agricultural Practices

Use Quality Inputs

Farmers should use appropriate and reliable agricultural inputs and follow relevant instructions and requirements.

Maintain Clean Production Areas

Clean and properly managed production areas can contribute to better agricultural hygiene and product quality.

Proper Handling

Agricultural products should be handled carefully during harvesting, processing, storage and transportation.

Record Keeping

Proper records can help farmers and businesses monitor production, identify challenges and improve management.

Continuous Improvement

Farmers and agricultural businesses are encouraged to continuously improve their production and handling practices.

Promoting Quality Agriculture

The Ministry is committed to supporting agricultural practices that promote quality, responsible production and stronger agricultural value chains across Kogi State.
