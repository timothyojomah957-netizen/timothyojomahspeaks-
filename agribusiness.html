Agribusiness & Entrepreneurship

Promoting agricultural entrepreneurship, private-sector participation and profitable agribusiness opportunities across the agricultural value chain in Kogi State.

Building Agricultural Businesses

Agriculture offers opportunities beyond farming. Agribusiness includes production, processing, transportation, storage, marketing, finance, technology and other services that support the agricultural value chain.

🌾

Farm Enterprises

Supporting farmers to view agricultural production as a business and improve planning, productivity and market orientation.

🏭

Agro-Processing

Encouraging businesses that transform agricultural raw materials into higher-value products.

🚛

Logistics

Transportation and logistics businesses can support the movement of agricultural inputs and products.

📦

Storage & Warehousing

Storage businesses can help protect agricultural products and support efficient market supply.

🛒

Agricultural Marketing

Marketing enterprises can connect producers with consumers, processors and commercial buyers.

💡

Agricultural Innovation

Technology and innovative business models can improve productivity, market access and agricultural services.

Agribusiness Opportunities

Crop Production

Commercial crop production can provide opportunities for farmers, investors and agricultural entrepreneurs.

Livestock Enterprise

Livestock production and related businesses can contribute to employment, food supply and income generation.

Input Supply

Businesses can provide farmers with appropriate agricultural inputs and services through reliable channels.

Processing & Packaging

Value addition creates opportunities for businesses to process and package agricultural products for different markets.

Digital Agriculture

Digital tools can create opportunities in agricultural information, marketing, finance and farm management.

Agricultural Services

Equipment rental, consultancy, transportation and other services can support farmers and agribusinesses.

Starting an Agricultural Enterprise

1. Identify an Opportunity

Identify a genuine agricultural need or market opportunity that can support a sustainable business.

2. Understand the Market

Research customers, competitors, prices, demand and other factors that may affect the business.

3. Develop a Business Plan

A clear business plan can help define objectives, resources, expected costs, income and growth strategies.

4. Access Appropriate Finance

Entrepreneurs should explore suitable and legitimate sources of finance according to the needs of their enterprise.

5. Build Strong Partnerships

Partnerships with farmers, cooperatives, processors, buyers and service providers can strengthen agricultural businesses.

Growing Agriculture Through Enterprise

Strong agribusinesses can create jobs, improve market access, encourage investment and help transform agriculture into a more competitive economic sector in Kogi State.
