Hon. Timothy Ojomah Urges Ibaji People to Remain Calm, Assures Them Government Has Not Forgotten Them

Published: August 25, 2026

The Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Hon. Timothy Ojomah, has urged the good people of Ibaji Local Government Area to remain calm, assuring them that the present administration has not forgotten them.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah made the call while reaffirming the commitment of the government to the development and wellbeing of the people of Ibaji. He acknowledged the concerns and expectations of the people and assured them that their needs remain part of the government's development priorities.

Government Remains Committed to Ibaji

According to the Commissioner, the people of Ibaji are an important part of Kogi State and deserve continued attention, support and meaningful development. He appealed to the people to remain patient and peaceful, stressing that development is a continuous process that requires cooperation between government and the people.

He further assured the people that the present government is aware of the challenges confronting communities in Ibaji and is working towards addressing them through appropriate programmes and interventions.

Agriculture as a Tool for Development

Hon. Timothy Ojomah also emphasized the importance of agriculture in improving the livelihoods of families and strengthening the economy of the local government. He noted that supporting farmers and encouraging productive agricultural activities remain important steps towards improving food security and creating economic opportunities.

He encouraged farmers and other agricultural stakeholders in Ibaji to remain focused and continue to support initiatives aimed at improving agricultural productivity and strengthening food security across Kogi State.

A Message of Hope to the People

The Commissioner called on the people of Ibaji to continue to have confidence in the government and to remain united as efforts are made to bring more development to the area.

He reiterated that the people of Ibaji have not been forgotten and that the government remains committed to working for the progress, peace and prosperity of the people.
