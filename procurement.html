Agricultural Procurement

Information about agricultural procurement, supplies, suppliers and transparent purchasing processes within the Ministry.

Procurement Areas

Agricultural procurement supports the delivery of programmes, projects and services through the acquisition of appropriate goods, works and services.

🌾

Agricultural Inputs

Procurement of approved agricultural inputs and materials required for agricultural programmes and activities.

🚜

Farm Equipment

Agricultural machinery, equipment and related materials may be procured to support agricultural development activities.

🏗️

Infrastructure

Procurement may include works and infrastructure required to support agricultural production and related programmes.

📦

Supplies

General supplies and materials required for the implementation of approved Ministry programmes and activities.

💼

Professional Services

Appropriate professional and technical services may be engaged according to approved procurement procedures.

🤝

Qualified Suppliers

Eligible suppliers and contractors may participate in procurement opportunities in accordance with applicable requirements.

Procurement Process

1

Procurement Planning

Identifying approved requirements and planning procurement activities according to the Ministry's programmes and budget.

2

Invitation & Advertisement

Where applicable, procurement opportunities are communicated through authorized channels.

3

Submission

Interested and eligible suppliers or contractors submit the required documents according to the stated requirements.

4

Evaluation

Submitted bids or proposals are evaluated according to applicable procurement requirements and criteria.

5

Award & Implementation

Successful procurement processes proceed to award and contract implementation in accordance with applicable rules.

Important Procurement Notice

Procurement opportunities, tender requirements, deadlines and award information should only be relied upon when officially published or confirmed through authorized government channels.

Transparency & Accountability

Procurement activities should promote fairness, transparency, accountability, value for money and compliance with applicable government procurement requirements.

Procurement Enquiries

For official information about procurement activities and requirements, contact the Kogi State Ministry of Agriculture through its authorized channels.
