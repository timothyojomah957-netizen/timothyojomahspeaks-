Agricultural Awards

Celebrating excellence, innovation, dedication and outstanding contributions to agricultural development in Kogi State.

Award Categories

Agricultural awards can recognize individuals, organizations and communities that make meaningful contributions to farming and agricultural development.

🏆

Outstanding Farmer

Recognition for farmers who demonstrate exceptional dedication, productivity and responsible agricultural practices.

🌾

Best Agricultural Producer

Recognition for outstanding performance in agricultural production and value-chain development.

💡

Agricultural Innovator

Recognition for innovative ideas, technologies and approaches that contribute to agricultural development.

👩‍🌾

Women in Agriculture

Recognition of outstanding contributions by women to farming, agribusiness and agricultural development.

👨‍🌾

Youth in Agriculture

Celebrating young people who demonstrate commitment and innovation within the agricultural sector.

🤝

Outstanding Cooperative

Recognition for cooperatives that demonstrate strong organization, productivity and community impact.

Recognition Criteria

Productivity

Demonstrated achievement and positive results in agricultural production or related activities.

Innovation

Use of creative ideas, improved techniques or appropriate technology to address agricultural challenges.

Community Impact

Contribution to farmers, communities, employment, food production or agricultural development.

Sustainability

Commitment to responsible practices that support long-term agricultural and environmental sustainability.

Leadership

Demonstrated leadership, cooperation and positive contribution to agricultural communities or organizations.

Celebrating Agricultural Excellence

Recognition and awards can encourage farmers, young people, women, cooperatives, businesses and other stakeholders to continue making positive contributions to the agricultural sector.

Promoting Excellence in Agriculture

The Kogi State Ministry of Agriculture supports initiatives that encourage productivity, innovation and sustainable agricultural development.
