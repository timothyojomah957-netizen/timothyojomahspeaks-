Agricultural Processing & Value Addition

Promoting the processing, preservation, packaging and transformation of agricultural products to create greater value, reduce losses and strengthen agricultural businesses across Kogi State.

Adding Value to Agricultural Products

Agricultural processing creates opportunities to transform raw farm products into useful and marketable products while supporting farmers, processors, entrepreneurs and other participants across the value chain.

🌽

Crop Processing

Supporting appropriate processing of crops into products suitable for consumption, storage and marketing.

🥜

Food Processing

Encouraging transformation of agricultural products into safe and useful food products.

🐄

Livestock Processing

Supporting appropriate handling and processing opportunities within livestock value chains.

🐟

Fish Processing

Promoting improved handling, preservation and processing of fish and fisheries products.

📦

Packaging

Encouraging appropriate packaging that can protect agricultural products and improve their market presentation.

🏭

Agro-Industries

Supporting opportunities for agricultural enterprises and industries involved in processing and value addition.

Benefits of Value Addition

Reduce Post-Harvest Losses

Processing and preservation can help farmers and businesses manage agricultural products after harvest.

Increase Product Value

Transforming raw agricultural products can create products with greater economic and market value.

Create Employment

Processing and value-added businesses can create opportunities for workers, entrepreneurs and young people.

Improve Market Access

Properly processed and packaged products can reach different markets and customers.

Support Farmers

Strong value chains can create additional opportunities for farmers to participate in agricultural businesses.

Strengthen the Economy

Agricultural processing can contribute to local enterprise growth and the wider agricultural economy.

Agricultural Value Chain

1. Production

Farmers produce crops, livestock, fisheries and other agricultural commodities.

2. Collection

Agricultural products are collected and organized for transportation, storage or processing.

3. Processing

Raw agricultural products are transformed into processed or semi-processed products.

4. Packaging & Storage

Products are properly packaged and stored to maintain quality and improve their shelf life.

5. Marketing

Finished products are connected with consumers, businesses and different markets.

Promoting Agricultural Value Addition

Developing stronger processing and value chains can help create employment, improve farmer incomes, reduce losses and strengthen the agricultural economy of Kogi State.
