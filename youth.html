Youth in Agriculture

Empowering young people to participate in agriculture, agribusiness, innovation, food production and agricultural value chains across Kogi State.

Opportunities for Young People

Agriculture offers young people opportunities to develop skills, establish businesses, create employment and contribute to food security.

🌱

Modern Farming

Young people can explore modern approaches to crop production, livestock, fisheries and other agricultural activities.

💼

Agribusiness

Opportunities exist across agricultural production, processing, marketing, logistics and other parts of the value chain.

💻

Agri-Tech

Technology can help young entrepreneurs develop innovative solutions for farmers, markets and agricultural businesses.

📚

Training

Agricultural training and capacity building can help young people develop practical and entrepreneurial skills.

🏭

Agro-Processing

Value addition and processing can provide opportunities for young entrepreneurs to build sustainable businesses.

🤝

Partnerships

Collaboration with government, businesses and development partners can create pathways for youth participation in agriculture.

Key Areas of Youth Participation

Crop Production

Participation in commercial and small-scale crop farming and agricultural enterprises.

Livestock

Opportunities in livestock production, management and related agricultural businesses.

Fisheries

Youth participation in fish production, aquaculture and fisheries value chains.

Processing

Processing agricultural products into higher-value products for consumers and markets.

Marketing

Building businesses around agricultural distribution, marketing and market access.

Innovation

Developing new ideas and technologies that can solve challenges within the agricultural sector.

Getting Started

1. Choose an Area

Identify an agricultural area that matches your interests, skills and available resources.

2. Gain Knowledge

Seek training, agricultural information and practical experience.

3. Explore Opportunities

Check official programs, opportunities and partnerships that may be relevant to your agricultural plans.

4. Build Your Business

Develop a practical plan and look for ways to create value within the agricultural sector.
