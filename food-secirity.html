Food Security

Supporting increased agricultural production, sustainable food systems and improved access to safe and nutritious food across Kogi State.

Understanding Food Security

Food security means that people have reliable access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food. A strong agricultural sector plays an important role in supporting food availability, livelihoods and economic development.

🌾

Food Production

Supporting agricultural production of crops, livestock and other food products to strengthen local food supplies.

🥬

Nutritious Food

Encouraging diverse agricultural production that contributes to healthy and nutritious diets.

👨‍🌾

Farmer Livelihoods

Supporting farmers and agricultural workers can strengthen livelihoods and contribute to sustainable food systems.

🏪

Food Markets

Efficient agricultural markets help connect producers with consumers and support the movement of food products.

🌱

Sustainable Agriculture

Responsible farming practices can help protect natural resources while supporting long-term food production.

🚜

Modern Agriculture

Appropriate technology, knowledge and improved farming practices can contribute to efficient agricultural production.

Key Areas of Food Security

Food Availability

Agricultural production and effective food supply systems can contribute to making sufficient food available to communities.

Food Access

Strong agricultural livelihoods and functioning markets can help improve people's ability to access food.

Food Safety and Quality

Proper handling, storage and processing of agricultural products can contribute to safer and better-quality food.

Stable Food Supply

Resilient agricultural systems can help communities respond to changing weather conditions and other production challenges.

Supporting Food Security in Kogi State

Support Farmers

Strengthening farmers through agricultural information, training, appropriate inputs and support programmes can improve production.

Improve Productivity

Better farming practices, appropriate mechanization and improved technologies can contribute to higher agricultural productivity.

Reduce Post-Harvest Losses

Better harvesting, storage, transportation and processing practices can help reduce avoidable losses of agricultural products.

Strengthen Agricultural Markets

Connecting farmers with buyers and markets can support agricultural businesses and improve food supply chains.

Promote Youth Participation

Opportunities for young people in farming, agribusiness and agricultural technology can contribute to a stronger food system.

Encourage Sustainable Practices

Responsible management of soil, water and other agricultural resources supports long-term food production.

Important Notice

Food security depends on the combined efforts of farmers, consumers, agricultural businesses, government institutions, communities and other stakeholders. Farmers should verify official programme and support information through appropriate Ministry channels.

Building a Stronger Food System

The Ministry supports efforts that strengthen agricultural production, farmer livelihoods and sustainable food systems across Kogi State.
