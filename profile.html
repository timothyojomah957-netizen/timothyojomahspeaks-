Hon. Timothy Ojomah

Commissioner of Agriculture and Food Security, Kogi State

About the Commissioner

Hon. Timothy Ojomah is the Commissioner of Agriculture and Food Security in Kogi State. He is committed to advancing agriculture, supporting farmers and promoting food security across the state.

His focus includes farmer empowerment, agricultural development, youth participation in agriculture and initiatives aimed at strengthening food production and the agricultural value chain.

Vision and Commitment

His vision is to contribute to a stronger and more productive agricultural sector in Kogi State by supporting farmers, encouraging modern agricultural practices and creating opportunities within the agricultural sector.

Key Areas of Focus

Farmer Empowerment: Supporting farmers with opportunities, knowledge and resources.

Food Security: Promoting agricultural initiatives that contribute to increased food production.

Youth in Agriculture: Encouraging young people to participate in agriculture and agricultural businesses.

Agricultural Development: Promoting sustainable agricultural development throughout Kogi State.
