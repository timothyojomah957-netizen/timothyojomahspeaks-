About Hon. Timothy Ojomah

Hon. Timothy Ojomah is a public servant and agricultural development advocate serving as the Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security in Kogi State.

He is committed to strengthening the agricultural sector, improving food production, empowering farmers and creating opportunities for young people to participate meaningfully in agriculture.

His focus is on agriculture as a driver of food security, employment creation, rural development and economic growth in Kogi State.

Agricultural Development

Under his leadership at the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security, emphasis has been placed on modern agricultural practices, farmer support, agricultural mechanisation, youth empowerment and initiatives aimed at improving food security across Kogi State.

Youth Empowerment

One of the areas receiving attention is the development of young people through modern agribusiness. Training initiatives have exposed Kogi youths to emerging agricultural practices including greenhouse management, hydroponics and aeroponics.

These initiatives are designed to help young people acquire practical agricultural skills that can translate into employment opportunities and sustainable livelihoods.

Supporting Farmers

Hon. Ojomah has also been involved in efforts to expand agricultural production and strengthen support for farmers across the state.

His approach recognises agriculture not only as a means of producing food, but also as an important avenue for employment creation, rural development and economic growth.

Agricultural Value Chains

The ministry has also focused attention on important agricultural value chains, including rice, maize, cassava and cashew, while seeking stronger connections between farmers, markets, investors and other stakeholders in the agricultural sector.

Vision for Kogi Agriculture

His vision for Kogi agriculture is centred on a more productive and commercially viable agricultural sector where farmers have better access to opportunities, young people can build careers in agribusiness, and the state can make meaningful progress towards food security.

Commitment to Service

Through his service as Commissioner, Hon. Timothy Ojomah continues to champion the idea that agriculture can be a major driver of Kogi State's economic transformation and a pathway to greater prosperity for its people.
