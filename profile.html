Hon. Timothy Ojomah

Commissioner of Agriculture and Food Security, Kogi State

About the Commissioner

Hon. Timothy Ojomah is the Commissioner of Agriculture and Food Security in Kogi State. He is committed to the advancement of agriculture, farmer empowerment and food security across the state.

His work focuses on strengthening agricultural development, supporting farmers, encouraging youth participation in agriculture and promoting initiatives that contribute to sustainable food production.

Vision and Commitment

His vision is to contribute to a stronger and more productive agricultural sector in Kogi State by supporting farmers, promoting modern agricultural practices and creating opportunities within the agricultural value chain.

Key Areas of Focus

Farmer Empowerment

Supporting farmers with opportunities, knowledge and resources to improve agricultural productivity.

Food Security

Promoting agricultural initiatives that can increase food production and strengthen food security.

Youth in Agriculture

Encouraging young people to participate in agriculture and develop sustainable agricultural enterprises.

Agricultural Development

Promoting sustainable agricultural development and improved opportunities across Kogi State.
