Our Mission

Our mission is to promote sustainable agriculture, improve food production and strengthen the livelihood of farmers through practical agricultural development initiatives.

We are committed to supporting farmers with access to modern agricultural practices, opportunities, improved productivity and programmes that contribute to food security and economic growth.

Through collaboration with farmers, communities, government institutions and other stakeholders, we seek to create an agricultural sector that benefits the people and contributes meaningfully to the development of Kogi State.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a strong, productive and sustainable agricultural sector where farmers are empowered, food security is strengthened and agriculture becomes a major driver of economic development.

We envision an agricultural system where the hardworking people of Kogi State can turn their farms into sustainable sources of income while contributing to the availability of food for present and future generations.

Commitment to Farmers

We remain committed to listening to farmers, understanding the challenges they face and supporting initiatives that can improve agricultural productivity and rural livelihoods.
