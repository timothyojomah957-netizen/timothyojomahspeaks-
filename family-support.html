Youth & Family Farming Support

Encouraging young people and families to participate in agriculture, develop practical skills and build sustainable farming and agribusiness opportunities across Kogi State.

Supporting the Next Generation of Farmers

Young people and farming families are important to the future of food production, rural development and agricultural transformation.

Building Skills and Opportunities

Agricultural education and practical training can help young people understand modern farming, agribusiness, technology and opportunities across agricultural value chains.


Family farming can also provide an important foundation for passing agricultural knowledge from one generation to another while creating opportunities for food production, income generation and entrepreneurship.

Youth Farming Opportunities

Agriculture provides opportunities beyond traditional crop production. Young people can participate in different parts of the agricultural value chain.

🌱

Crop Production

Young farmers can develop skills in modern crop production and farm management.

🐄

Livestock

Opportunities exist in livestock production, management and related agribusiness activities.

🐟

Aquaculture

Fish farming can provide opportunities for young entrepreneurs in production, processing and marketing.

📱

Agri-Tech

Technology can help young people develop innovative solutions for modern agriculture.

🏭

Agro-Processing

Young entrepreneurs can participate in processing, packaging and value addition.

🛒

Marketing

Agricultural marketing and distribution provide additional opportunities throughout the value chain.

Family Farming Support

Family farms can combine traditional knowledge with modern agricultural practices to improve productivity and create sustainable livelihoods.

1

Share Agricultural Knowledge

Families can pass farming knowledge between generations while adopting improved production techniques.

2

Encourage Youth Participation

Parents and farming communities can encourage young people to view agriculture as a productive career and business opportunity.

3

Adopt Modern Practices

Families can improve farm productivity through appropriate technology, better farm management and sustainable practices.

4

Build Family Agribusinesses

Farming families can develop enterprises around production, processing, storage, transportation and marketing.

5

Plan for the Future

Long-term planning can help families maintain productive farms and create opportunities for future generations.

Building the Future Through Agriculture

Agriculture as a Career and Business

Young people can contribute to the transformation of agriculture by combining farming knowledge with education, entrepreneurship, technology and innovation. Family farms can provide a practical environment where agricultural skills are developed and passed to the next generation.

Empowering Young Farmers and Families

A strong agricultural future depends on skilled young people, productive families and communities that see agriculture as an important pathway to food security and economic opportunity.
