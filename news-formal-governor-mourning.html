Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security Hon. Timothy Ojoma Mourns the Passing of a Former Governor of Kogi State

📅 Published: 20 September 2026

A HEARTFELT TRIBUTE TO A FORMER GOVERNOR OF KOGI STATE

I received with a very heavy heart the sad news of the passing of our former Governor and a prominent son of Kogi State.

Words are truly inadequate to describe how deeply saddened I am by this loss. Although death is a reality that awaits every mortal, it is never easy to receive the news of the passing of someone who has been part of the history, growth, and journey of our dear state.

For me, this is not just the passing of a former Governor. It is the loss of a fellow Kogite, a leader, a father, a brother, and a man whose life was connected in one way or another to the story of our people.

I cannot think about his passing without reflecting on the years he spent serving our people and the responsibilities he carried during his time in public service. Leadership comes with enormous challenges, and those who accept the responsibility of serving the people leave behind a part of themselves in the communities they lead.

Today, my heart goes out first to his family.

To his wife, children, relatives, friends, and loved ones, I want you to know that my thoughts and prayers are with you at this incredibly difficult time. I know that no words can completely take away the pain of losing someone so dear. I pray, however, that the memories of the beautiful moments you shared with him will continue to give you strength and comfort.

There will be moments when his absence will be deeply felt—at family gatherings, in conversations, in familiar places, and in the memories of the years you shared together. May God give you the grace to hold on to those memories and find peace in knowing that his life had meaning and that he touched many lives during his time on earth.

As Commissioner for Agriculture, I join the good people of Kogi State in mourning this painful loss. But beyond my official capacity, I mourn as a fellow citizen and as someone who understands that behind every public office is a human being, a family, relationships, hopes, dreams, struggles, and a lifetime of memories.

His passing reminds us all of the temporary nature of our time here on earth. It reminds us that positions, titles, wealth, and influence are all temporary, but the kindness we show, the people we help, and the positive footprints we leave behind can live on long after we are gone.

At this moment of grief, I choose to remember him with respect and gratitude for the role he played in the history of our state.

May Almighty God, in His infinite mercy, forgive his shortcomings, accept his soul, and grant him eternal rest.

May God comfort his family. May He strengthen his children and loved ones. May He give his friends and associates the courage to continue without him. And may He grant the people of Kogi State the grace to preserve the lessons and memories that his life has left with us.

Your Excellency, your earthly journey has come to an end, but your memory will remain.

May you rest peacefully in the bosom of the Almighty.

My deepest condolences to the family and the entire people of Kogi State.

Hon. Timothy Ojoma
Commissioner for Agriculture, Kogi State
