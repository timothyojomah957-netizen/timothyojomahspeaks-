Commissioner Launches New Agricultural Programme

Published: August 23, 2026

The Kogi State Ministry of Agriculture continues to strengthen agricultural development through programmes and initiatives aimed at supporting farmers and improving food production across the state.

The initiative is part of ongoing efforts to create greater opportunities for farmers, encourage modern agricultural practices and contribute to food security in Kogi State.

Supporting Farmers

The Ministry remains committed to supporting farmers and creating an environment where agricultural activities can contribute to improved livelihoods and economic development.

Building a Stronger Agricultural Future

Through collaboration with farmers, communities and other stakeholders, the Ministry will continue to promote sustainable agriculture and encourage greater participation in the agricultural sector.
