Agricultural Development and Farmer Support in Kogi State

Kogi State Ministry of Agriculture

The Kogi State Ministry of Agriculture continues to promote agricultural development and support initiatives aimed at improving the lives of farmers across the state.

Through strategic agricultural programmes, farmer support, empowerment initiatives and partnerships, the Ministry is working towards a stronger and more sustainable agricultural sector.

Supporting Farmers

Supporting farmers remains an important part of the agricultural development agenda. The Ministry continues to encourage modern farming practices, increased food production and opportunities for farmers and young people interested in agriculture.

Building Food Security

These efforts are also aimed at strengthening food production and contributing to food security and economic development across Kogi State.
