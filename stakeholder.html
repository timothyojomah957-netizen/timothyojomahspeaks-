Agricultural Stakeholders

Working together with farmers, cooperatives, businesses, development partners and communities to strengthen agriculture across Kogi State.

Our Stakeholders

Agricultural development depends on strong collaboration among government, farmers, private organizations, communities and development partners.

👨‍🌾

Farmers

Farmers are central to agricultural production and food security. Their knowledge, experience and participation are important to agricultural development.

🤝

Farmers' Cooperatives

Cooperatives can help farmers work together, improve access to markets, share knowledge and strengthen agricultural value chains.

🏢

Private Sector

Private businesses contribute through investment, agricultural services, processing, technology, logistics and market development.

🌍

Development Partners

Development organizations can support agricultural programs, technical assistance, capacity building and community development.

🏘️

Local Communities

Communities play an important role in agricultural activities, local development and the sustainability of agricultural initiatives.

🎓

Research & Academic Institutions

Research institutions and academic organizations contribute knowledge, innovation, research and agricultural education.

🏛️

Government Agencies

Government institutions work together to coordinate policies, programs, services and agricultural development initiatives.

💡

Technology & Innovation Partners

Technology organizations can support modern farming, digital agriculture, data management and agricultural innovation.

Areas of Collaboration

Farmer Empowerment

Supporting farmers through training, agricultural services, knowledge sharing and access to opportunities.

Food Security

Working together to improve agricultural productivity and contribute to food availability.

Investment

Encouraging responsible investment in agricultural production, processing and value-chain development.

Technology

Promoting the adoption of appropriate technologies and innovative approaches to agriculture.

Research

Supporting research, knowledge exchange and evidence-based agricultural development.

Market Access

Strengthening connections between producers, processors, businesses and agricultural markets.

Let's Work Together

The Ministry welcomes constructive engagement and collaboration with relevant stakeholders committed to agricultural development, farmer empowerment and food security in Kogi State.
