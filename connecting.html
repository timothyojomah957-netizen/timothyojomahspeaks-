Connecting Farmers with Buyers

Helping create opportunities for farmers and agricultural buyers to connect, exchange information and explore legitimate agricultural market opportunities across Kogi State.

Farmer–Buyer Connection

Connecting farmers with potential buyers can help improve access to markets, strengthen agricultural value chains and create opportunities for farmers to market their produce.

👨‍🌾

For Farmers

Farmers can provide information about their agricultural products, production capacity and preferred markets.

🏪

For Buyers

Buyers can indicate the agricultural products they are interested in sourcing and provide their contact information.

🌾

Agricultural Products

Produce may include crops, livestock products, fisheries products and other legitimate agricultural commodities.

🤝

Market Connections

Information can help farmers and buyers identify possible opportunities for communication and business discussions.

How the Connection Works

1. Farmer Provides Information

Farmers provide information about their products, location, estimated quantity and preferred contact method.

2. Buyer Provides Requirements

Buyers provide information about the products they are looking for, quantity and preferred location.

3. Information Is Reviewed

Submitted information should be reviewed for appropriate agricultural and market-related purposes.

4. Parties Communicate

Where appropriate, farmers and buyers may communicate directly to discuss prices, quantities, quality, delivery and other terms.

Strengthening Agricultural Markets

Better connections between farmers, buyers, processors and agricultural businesses can contribute to stronger value chains and improved market opportunities.
