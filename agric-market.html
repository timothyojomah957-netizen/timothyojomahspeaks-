Agricultural Value Chain

Agricultural products pass through several stages before reaching the final consumer.

Production

Farmers produce crops, livestock and fish.

Aggregation

Products are collected from individual producers.

Processing

Raw products can be processed and transformed.

Transportation

Products are moved between farms, markets and businesses.

Marketing

Products are promoted and connected with buyers.

Consumers

Finished products reach consumers and communities.

Market Development Support

Farmer Market Access

Supporting opportunities that can help farmers connect with buyers and agricultural markets.

Value Addition

Encouraging processing, packaging and other activities that can increase the value of agricultural products.

Market Information

Promoting access to useful information that can assist farmers and businesses in making market decisions.

Private Sector Participation

Encouraging private businesses and investors to participate in agricultural value chains.

Cooperative Marketing

Supporting farmer groups and cooperatives to strengthen collective production and marketing activities.

Market Infrastructure

Supporting improved infrastructure and systems that can facilitate agricultural trade and distribution.

Market Information Notice: Agricultural prices and market statistics should be updated with verified and current information before publication. Unverified prices should not be presented as official market prices.

Strengthening Agricultural Markets

Strong agricultural markets can create opportunities for farmers, businesses and communities while contributing to agricultural growth and food security.
