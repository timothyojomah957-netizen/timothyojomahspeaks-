Empowering Women and Youth in Agriculture

Agriculture provides important opportunities for employment, entrepreneurship, food production and economic development. This page provides information about possible agricultural funding and empowerment opportunities for women and young people.

Women in Agriculture

Women can participate in crop production, livestock, poultry, fisheries, agro-processing, agricultural trading and other agribusiness activities.

🌾 Crop Farming

Opportunities for women involved in crop production and agricultural enterprises.

🐔 Poultry

Support opportunities for women interested in poultry production and related businesses.

🐟 Fisheries

Opportunities for women participating in fish farming, fisheries and aquaculture.

🏭 Agro-Processing

Encouraging women to participate in agricultural processing and value-added agricultural businesses.

Youth in Agriculture

Young people can explore agriculture as a career, business and source of employment through farming, agribusiness, technology, processing and agricultural services.

🚜 Modern Farming

Encouraging young farmers to adopt modern farming practices and technologies.

💼 Agribusiness

Supporting young people interested in agricultural businesses, processing and marketing.

🎓 Training

Encouraging agricultural education, practical training and entrepreneurship development.

💡 Agricultural Innovation

Promoting innovation and technology-based opportunities within the agricultural sector.

Possible Areas of Support

  • Agricultural loans
  • Grant opportunities
  • Farm inputs
  • Agricultural equipment
  • Training and skills development
  • Cooperative support
  • Agro-processing opportunities
  • Agricultural entrepreneurship
  • Market and value-chain opportunities

General Eligibility Requirements

Specific requirements depend on the particular programme. Applicants may generally be required to provide accurate personal information, evidence of agricultural activity and other documents specified by the programme.

  • Applicant should meet the age or programme requirements.
  • Applicant should provide accurate information.
  • Applicant may need to demonstrate involvement in agriculture.
  • Some programmes may require cooperative membership.
  • Applicants must satisfy the requirements of the specific programme.

Interested in Agricultural Funding?

Farmers, women and young people who want to enquire about available agricultural funding can submit a funding enquiry.
