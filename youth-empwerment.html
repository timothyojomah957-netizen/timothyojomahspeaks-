Youth Empowerment in Agriculture

Creating opportunities for young people to participate in farming, agribusiness, agricultural technology, skills development and the wider agricultural value chain.

Empowering the Next Generation

Young people have an important role to play in the future of agriculture. Through skills, innovation, entrepreneurship and access to opportunities, youth can contribute to a stronger agricultural economy.

🌱

Young Farmers

Encouraging young people to participate in modern agricultural production and develop sustainable farm enterprises.

💼

Agribusiness

Supporting young entrepreneurs interested in agricultural processing, marketing, logistics, input supply and other value-chain activities.

📱

Agri-Tech

Encouraging young innovators to explore technology and digital solutions for agricultural challenges.

🎓

Skills Development

Promoting practical agricultural knowledge and entrepreneurship skills that can help young people build sustainable livelihoods.

🤝

Mentorship

Connecting young agricultural entrepreneurs with experienced farmers, professionals and relevant agricultural stakeholders.

🚜

Modern Agriculture

Introducing young people to modern production methods, mechanization, processing and other agricultural technologies.

Youth Agricultural Opportunities

Crop Production

Young entrepreneurs can explore commercial crop production and related agricultural enterprises.

Livestock

Livestock production and supporting services provide opportunities for youth participation in agriculture.

Agro-Processing

Processing and packaging agricultural products can create jobs and add value to farm produce.

Digital Agriculture

Technology creates opportunities in agricultural information, marketing, farm management and digital services.

Farm Services

Equipment rental, transportation, extension support and other agricultural services can create youth businesses.

Marketing

Young people can develop businesses connecting farmers with consumers, processors and commercial markets.

Pathways to Agricultural Success

1. Learn

Develop practical knowledge and skills relevant to the chosen agricultural enterprise.

2. Identify an Opportunity

Study the agricultural value chain and identify a realistic business or employment opportunity.

3. Start Small

Begin with a manageable agricultural enterprise and grow gradually as experience and resources increase.

4. Build Networks

Work with farmer groups, cooperatives, mentors, businesses and other agricultural stakeholders.

5. Embrace Innovation

Use appropriate technology, improved practices and creative business ideas to increase efficiency and competitiveness.

Young People, Stronger Agriculture

Empowering young people in agriculture can create employment, encourage innovation, strengthen food production and build a new generation of agricultural entrepreneurs in Kogi State.
