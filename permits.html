General Permit Process

1. Identify the Permit

Determine the specific permit or authorization that may apply to the intended agricultural activity.

2. Check Requirements

Obtain current information about the applicable documents, conditions and requirements.

3. Submit Application

Complete and submit the required application through the appropriate Ministry office or authorized channel.

4. Review

The application and supporting information may be reviewed by the relevant authority.

5. Approval or Further Guidance

Applicants may receive approval, documentation or additional instructions depending on the applicable process.

Important Information

Permit requirements differ according to the activity involved. Applicants should contact the appropriate Ministry office to confirm current requirements before applying.
