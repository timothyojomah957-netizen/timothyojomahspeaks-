Agricultural Research

Promoting agricultural research, knowledge and innovation to support productive, sustainable and resilient agriculture across Kogi State.

Why Agricultural Research Matters

Agricultural research can help identify better farming practices, improve productivity, address agricultural challenges and support evidence-based decisions within the agricultural sector.

🌱

Crop Research

Research can support improved crop production practices, crop management and responsible use of agricultural resources.

🐄

Livestock Research

Research can contribute to improved livestock management, animal production and sustainable husbandry practices.

🌾

Soil Research

Soil research can help improve understanding of soil conditions, fertility management and sustainable agricultural practices.

💧

Water & Irrigation

Research can support efficient water management and appropriate irrigation practices for agricultural production.

🌦️

Climate Research

Agricultural research can help farmers and planners understand climate-related risks and opportunities.

🚜

Farm Technology

Research and innovation can support the adoption of appropriate agricultural technologies and mechanization.

Research Focus Areas

Food Production

Supporting research that can contribute to improved food production and agricultural productivity.

Food Security

Research can help identify practical approaches to strengthening food availability and sustainable production.

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Research can contribute to farming practices that respond to changing environmental and climatic conditions.

Post-Harvest Management

Research can help identify ways of reducing losses and improving storage, handling and processing of agricultural products.

Agricultural Value Chains

Research can support better understanding of production, processing, transportation, marketing and other value-chain activities.

Innovation

New ideas and technologies can be studied and assessed for their suitability to local agricultural conditions.

From Research to Farming Practice

1. Identify an Agricultural Challenge

Farmers, agricultural officers and researchers can identify important challenges affecting agricultural production.

2. Study the Challenge

Appropriate research and data collection can be used to better understand the causes and possible solutions.

3. Test Appropriate Solutions

Potential farming practices, technologies or approaches can be evaluated under suitable conditions.

4. Share Useful Findings

Relevant research findings can be communicated through extension services, training, demonstrations and agricultural education.

5. Apply Suitable Practices

Farmers can consider appropriate recommendations based on their specific farming conditions and available resources.

Important Notice: Agricultural research findings and recommendations should be properly verified and interpreted by qualified professionals before being used for major production, financial or policy decisions.

Agricultural Research and Innovation

Research, reliable agricultural information and innovation can help strengthen farming, improve productivity and support the long-term development of agriculture in Kogi State.
