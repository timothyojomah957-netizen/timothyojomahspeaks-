Agricultural Markets

Supporting farmers with useful information about agricultural markets, buyers, value chains, marketing opportunities and responsible trading.

Understanding Agricultural Markets

Agricultural markets connect farmers with consumers, traders, processors and other businesses. Good market information can help farmers make better production and marketing decisions.

🌾

Crop Markets

Information about the marketing of crops such as rice, cassava, maize, yam and other agricultural products.

🐄

Livestock Markets

Agricultural markets also provide opportunities for farmers involved in livestock production and related value-chain activities.

🐟

Fisheries Markets

Fisheries and aquaculture products can reach consumers through appropriate marketing and distribution channels.

🏪

Local Markets

Local markets provide opportunities for farmers to sell agricultural products to consumers, traders and businesses.

🚚

Distribution

Efficient transportation, storage and distribution can help move agricultural products from farms to markets.

🤝

Buyer Connections

Strong relationships between farmers, buyers, processors and traders can support agricultural value chains.

Agricultural Value Chain

1. Production

Farmers produce crops, livestock, fish and other agricultural products for consumption or further processing.

2. Storage

Appropriate storage can help protect agricultural products and maintain their quality before sale.

3. Processing

Processing can add value to agricultural products and create additional business opportunities.

4. Transportation

Reliable transportation helps agricultural products move from farms and processing locations to markets.

5. Marketing

Farmers and agricultural businesses can identify suitable buyers and markets for their products.

6. Consumers

Agricultural products ultimately reach consumers through different market and distribution channels.

Useful Marketing Tips for Farmers

Know Your Market

Farmers should understand the type of buyers interested in their products and the requirements of their target market.

Compare Prices

Comparing available market information can help farmers make informed decisions about where and when to sell.

Maintain Product Quality

Proper harvesting, handling, storage and transportation can help preserve agricultural product quality.

Keep Records

Farmers should keep records of production costs, quantities sold, buyers and sales revenue.

Work With Cooperatives

Farmer groups and cooperatives may help members improve bargaining, marketing and access to buyers.

Important Notice: Market prices can change according to location, season, supply, demand, quality and other factors. Farmers should verify current prices and buyer information before making important financial decisions.

Connecting Farmers With Markets

Strong agricultural markets can create opportunities for farmers, processors, traders, investors and consumers across Kogi State.
