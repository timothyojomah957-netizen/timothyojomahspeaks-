Food Security

Building a stronger agricultural sector that supports food production, farmer livelihoods, nutrition and sustainable food security across Kogi State.

Agriculture and Food Security

Food security depends on productive farmers, sustainable agricultural practices, functioning markets, responsible resource management and effective support across the food system.

Leadership for Food Security

Under the leadership of Hon. Timothy Ojomah, Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, the Ministry's agricultural development agenda places food production, farmer empowerment and sustainable agricultural development at the centre of its activities.


Strengthening crop production, livestock, fisheries, agricultural education, value chains and farmer support can contribute to a more resilient food system for communities across Kogi State.

Key Areas of Food Security

A strong food system requires action across production, storage, processing, distribution and access to nutritious food.

🌾

Food Production

Supporting farmers to increase responsible and sustainable production of food crops and other agricultural products.

👨‍🌾

Farmer Empowerment

Improving access to agricultural knowledge, skills, opportunities and relevant support for farmers.

🚜

Modern Agriculture

Encouraging modern agricultural practices, technology and improved production methods.

📦

Storage

Promoting appropriate storage and handling practices that can help reduce food losses after harvest.

🏭

Processing

Supporting agricultural processing and value addition to improve the usefulness and marketability of farm products.

🛒

Food Markets

Strengthening connections between farmers, traders, processors and consumers within agricultural value chains.

Pillars of Food Security

1

Availability

Supporting agricultural production so that sufficient food can be produced and supplied to communities.

2

Access

Supporting farmers, markets and economic opportunities that can improve people's ability to access food.

3

Utilization

Promoting awareness of safe food handling, nutrition and responsible use of agricultural products.

4

Stability

Building resilient agricultural systems capable of responding to climate, market and other challenges.

From Farm to Table

Food security requires cooperation across the entire agricultural value chain.

🌱 Production

Farmers produce crops, livestock and fish for food and economic activities.

📦 Storage

Proper handling and storage help protect agricultural products after harvest.

🏭 Processing

Processing can improve shelf life, quality and value of agricultural products.

🚚 Distribution

Efficient movement of food products helps connect producers with markets and consumers.

🛒 Markets

Strong markets provide opportunities for farmers to sell their products.

🍲 Nutrition

Access to diverse and safe food supports healthy communities.

Our Food Security Vision

To contribute to a Kogi State where farmers are productive, agricultural value chains are strengthened, food losses are reduced and communities have improved access to safe and nutritious food.

Working Towards Food Security

Strong agriculture requires the participation of farmers, communities, businesses, development partners and government institutions.
