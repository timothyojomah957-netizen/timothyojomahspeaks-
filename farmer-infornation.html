Useful Tips for Farmers

Plan Before Planting

Farmers should plan their production activities, resources and expected market opportunities before starting a farming season.

Keep Farm Records

Keeping simple records of farm activities, expenses, production and sales can help farmers make better decisions.

Use Reliable Information

Farmers should seek agricultural advice from qualified extension personnel and other reliable sources.

Protect Farm Resources

Responsible management of soil, water, crops, livestock and other farm resources supports sustainable agriculture.

Explore Market Opportunities

Understanding demand, buyers, prices and value chains can help farmers make informed marketing decisions.

Stay Updated

Farmers should follow official agricultural announcements for programmes, training and other relevant opportunities.

Important Notice

Farmers should verify agricultural programmes, financial opportunities, input distribution and other government announcements through official Ministry channels before taking action.
