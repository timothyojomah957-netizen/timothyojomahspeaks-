Livestock Agriculture

Supporting livestock farmers, improving productivity, strengthening resilience and promoting sustainable livestock development across Kogi State.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah and Livestock Development

Livestock development forms an important part of agricultural transformation, food security, employment and rural economic development.

Leadership and Commitment

Hon. Timothy Ojomah, Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, serves as Chairman of the Kogi State L-PRES State Steering Committee. The project focuses on improving livestock productivity, resilience and commercialization in selected value chains.


The Ministry has also supported capacity-building activities designed to equip livestock farmers and extension agents with modern and climate-resilient livestock production methods.

Priority Livestock Value Chains

Kogi L-PRES identifies poultry, sheep and goat, and cattle value chains, with emphasis on beef production, as priority areas.

Poultry

Supporting poultry farmers and businesses across production and related value-chain activities.

Sheep

Goat

Supporting goat production and the development of productive livestock enterprises.

Cattle & Beef

Promoting improved cattle production and opportunities within the beef value chain.
