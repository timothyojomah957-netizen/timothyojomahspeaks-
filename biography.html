BIOGRAPHY

Hon. Timothy Ojomah

Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Kogi State

About Hon. Timothy Ojomah

Hon. Timothy Ojomah serves as the Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security in Kogi State. In this capacity, he provides leadership and direction for the development of agriculture and food security initiatives across the state.

His portfolio focuses on strengthening agricultural productivity, supporting farmers, promoting modern farming practices and encouraging investment in the agricultural sector.

The Ministry under his portfolio works toward improving food production, supporting agricultural value chains and creating opportunities for farmers, young people and agricultural businesses.

Leadership and Responsibilities

  • Providing leadership for agricultural development.
  • Promoting food security across Kogi State.
  • Supporting farmers and agricultural communities.
  • Encouraging agricultural mechanisation.
  • Promoting youth participation in agriculture.
  • Supporting agricultural value-chain development.
  • Encouraging investment and partnerships in agriculture.

Focus on Agriculture

The Ministry's agricultural agenda is focused on building a productive and sustainable agricultural sector capable of contributing to food security, employment creation and economic development in Kogi State.

Core Areas of Service

  • Food Security
  • Farmer Support
  • Agricultural Development
  • Youth Empowerment
  • Modern Farming
  • Agricultural Investment
  • Sustainable Agriculture

Serving Kogi State

Through the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security, efforts continue toward creating a stronger agricultural sector and improving opportunities for farmers and communities across Kogi State.
