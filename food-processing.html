Food Processing & Value Addition

Promoting the processing, packaging and value addition of agricultural products to reduce post-harvest losses, create employment and strengthen agricultural businesses across Kogi State.

Adding Value to Agriculture

Food processing transforms agricultural raw materials into products that can have longer shelf lives, improved marketability and greater economic value.

🌾

Grain Processing

Processing grains into suitable food and commercial products can create additional value for farmers and processors.

🥜

Oilseed Processing

Oil-producing crops can be processed into oils and other useful products for household and commercial markets.

🍅

Fruit & Vegetable Processing

Appropriate processing can help extend the usability of fruits and vegetables and reduce losses after harvest.

🥛

Livestock Products

Appropriate processing and packaging of livestock products can support improved market opportunities.

📦

Packaging

Suitable packaging can protect processed products and improve their presentation, storage and marketability.

🏭

Agro-Processing

Agro-processing businesses can connect farmers with wider markets while creating opportunities for employment and investment.

Benefits of Value Addition

Reduce Post-Harvest Losses

Processing can help preserve agricultural products and reduce unnecessary losses after harvest.

Increase Product Value

Turning raw agricultural products into processed goods can create additional economic value.

Create Employment

Processing activities can create opportunities across production, packaging, transportation and marketing.

Expand Markets

Processed agricultural products can reach different consumer and commercial markets.

Support Farmers

Stronger processing industries can create additional demand for agricultural raw materials.

Support Food Security

Appropriate food processing can contribute to the availability and preservation of agricultural food products.

Agricultural Value Chain

1. Production

Farmers produce crops, livestock and other agricultural commodities that serve as raw materials for processing.

2. Collection

Agricultural products are collected and transported to suitable storage or processing locations.

3. Processing

Raw agricultural products are transformed into food or other value-added products using appropriate methods and equipment.

4. Packaging

Processed products are appropriately packaged to support protection, storage, transportation and marketing.

5. Marketing

Finished products are promoted and distributed through suitable markets and business channels.

Building a Stronger Agro-Processing Sector

Food processing and value addition can help transform agriculture into a stronger economic sector while creating opportunities for farmers, entrepreneurs, processors and communities across Kogi State.
