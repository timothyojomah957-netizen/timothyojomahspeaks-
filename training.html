Training & Capacity Building

Developing agricultural knowledge, practical skills and professional capacity for farmers, young people, women, cooperatives and other participants in Kogi State's agricultural sector.

Building Agricultural Skills

Training and capacity-building initiatives can help farmers and agricultural stakeholders improve their knowledge, adopt better practices and take advantage of emerging opportunities.

🌾

Farmer Training

Practical training on crop production, farm management and improved agricultural practices.

👨‍🌾

Young Farmers

Building the skills and knowledge of young people interested in agriculture and agribusiness.

👩‍🌾

Women in Agriculture

Supporting women with practical agricultural knowledge and entrepreneurship skills.

🤝

Cooperative Training

Strengthening the management, leadership and business skills of agricultural cooperatives.

🚜

Mechanization Skills

Promoting safe and appropriate use of agricultural machinery and modern farm equipment.

💼

Agribusiness Skills

Supporting knowledge in agricultural marketing, value addition, business planning and entrepreneurship.

Who Can Participate?

Farmers

Farmers seeking improved production knowledge and practical skills.

Youth

Young people interested in farming, agribusiness and agricultural entrepreneurship.

Women

Women participating in crop production, livestock, processing and other agricultural activities.

Cooperatives

Farmer groups and cooperatives seeking stronger organizational and business skills.

Key Training Areas

Modern Farming Practices

Training on appropriate and improved methods for crop and livestock production.

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Building awareness of agricultural practices that can improve resilience to changing climate conditions.

Post-Harvest Management

Improving knowledge of storage, handling, processing and reduction of post-harvest losses.

Marketing & Value Addition

Helping participants understand agricultural markets, processing, packaging and value-chain opportunities.

Farm Business Management

Developing skills in planning, record keeping, budgeting and managing agricultural enterprises.

Digital Agriculture

Introducing farmers and agricultural stakeholders to appropriate digital tools and information resources.

Empowering People Through Knowledge

Strong agricultural skills and knowledge can help create more productive farms, stronger agribusinesses and better opportunities for communities across Kogi State.
