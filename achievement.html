Agricultural Achievements

Highlighting agricultural development efforts, farmer support initiatives and progress towards a stronger and more productive agricultural sector in Kogi State.

Key Areas of Progress

The Ministry continues to focus on initiatives that strengthen farmers, improve agricultural productivity, support food security and promote sustainable agricultural development across Kogi State.

🌾

Support for Farmers

Strengthening communication with farmers and improving access to relevant agricultural information, programmes and support services.

🎓

Farmer Education

Promoting agricultural education, training, extension activities and knowledge-sharing opportunities for farmers.

🚜

Modern Agriculture

Promoting awareness of modern farming practices, appropriate mechanization and agricultural technologies.

👩‍🌾

Women in Agriculture

Supporting greater participation of women in agricultural activities, farming enterprises and agricultural opportunities.

👨‍🌾

Youth Participation

Encouraging young people to participate in agriculture and explore farming and agribusiness opportunities.

🌱

Food Security

Supporting agricultural activities that contribute to increased food production and food security in the state.

Agricultural Development Focus

Improved Agricultural Productivity

Promoting appropriate farming practices, improved knowledge and technologies that can help farmers improve productivity.

Farmer Support Programmes

Connecting eligible farmers with relevant agricultural programmes, training opportunities and support initiatives.

Agricultural Value Chains

Encouraging activities that strengthen production, processing, marketing and other parts of agricultural value chains.

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Promoting responsible agricultural practices that help farmers manage environmental and climate-related challenges.

Agricultural Investment

Encouraging investment and partnerships that can contribute to agricultural development and economic opportunities.

Expected Impact

Agricultural development initiatives are intended to create positive outcomes for farmers, communities and the wider economy.

Farmers

Better access to agricultural information, training and opportunities.

Communities

Stronger agricultural activities and improved rural economic opportunities.

Food Production

Support for increased agricultural production and food availability.

Youth & Women

Greater opportunities for participation in agriculture and agribusiness.
