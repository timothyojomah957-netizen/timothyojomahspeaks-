KOGI STATE • 35TH ANNIVERSARY

KOGI STATE @35: COMMISSIONER FOR AGRICULTURE AND FOOD SECURITY CELEBRATES 35 YEARS OF UNITY, RESILIENCE AND PROGRESS

📅 Published: August 27, 2026

As Kogi State commemorates the 35th anniversary of its creation, the Honourable Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Hon. Timothy Ojomah, extends warm congratulations to the Government and the good people of Kogi State on this historic milestone.

The 35th anniversary is an important opportunity to reflect on the journey of Kogi State, celebrate achievements and recognise the resilience, unity and determination of the people who have contributed to the growth and development of the state.

🌾 Agriculture remains a major pillar of Kogi State's development, food security, economic growth and empowerment of our people.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah reaffirmed the importance of supporting farmers, improving food production and creating sustainable opportunities for the people of Kogi State.

As Kogi State celebrates 35 years, the Commissioner calls for continued peace, unity, cooperation and commitment towards building a stronger and more prosperous state.

On this special occasion, Hon. Timothy Ojomah wishes the Government and the entire people of Kogi State a joyful 35th anniversary celebration.

Happy 35th Anniversary to Kogi State! 🎉🌾
May Kogi State continue to grow, prosper and achieve greater heights.
