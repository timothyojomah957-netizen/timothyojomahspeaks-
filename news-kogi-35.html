KOGI STATE @35: COMMISSIONER FOR AGRICULTURE AND FOOD SECURITY COMMENDS GOVERNMENT AND PEOPLE ON 35 YEARS OF UNITY, RESILIENCE AND PROGRESS

Published: August 27, 2026

As Kogi State commemorates the 35th anniversary of its creation, the Honourable Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security extends his warm congratulations to the Government and people of Kogi State on this significant milestone.

This anniversary provides an opportunity to reflect on the state's journey, acknowledge the sacrifices and contributions of those who have worked diligently to build Kogi State, and renew our collective commitment to a more prosperous and sustainable future.

Over the past 35 years, Kogi State has demonstrated the strength, resilience, diversity and determination of its people. From urban centres to rural communities, citizens have remained committed to fostering a state where peace, development and opportunity can thrive.

With its vast agricultural land, hardworking farmers, abundant natural resources and favourable conditions for agricultural production, agriculture remains a key pillar of Kogi State's economic future.

The Commissioner therefore reaffirms his commitment to supporting initiatives that advance food security, agricultural productivity, farmer empowerment, modern farming practices and sustainable agricultural development across Kogi State.

The state's farmers deserve recognition for their dedication and sacrifices. Every day, they work to provide food for families and sustain the economic life of communities.

As we celebrate Kogi State at 35, we must also look ahead with renewed optimism. The next phase of our development will require stronger collaboration among government, farmers, private investors, community leaders, youths, women and other stakeholders.

We must continue to encourage young people to view agriculture not merely as a means of livelihood, but as a viable business, career and investment opportunity.

Through innovation, technology, access to knowledge, improved inputs and stronger value chains, we can unlock the immense agricultural potential of our state.

This anniversary also reminds us that development is a collective responsibility. Every citizen has a role to play in building a peaceful, productive and prosperous Kogi State.

On this special occasion, the Commissioner celebrates the farmers, women, youths, civil servants, entrepreneurs, artisans, professionals, traditional rulers, community leaders and every citizen whose daily contributions continue to advance Kogi State.

We honour the past, appreciate the present and look to the future with confidence.

May this 35th anniversary strengthen the bonds of unity among our people and inspire renewed commitment to the development of our state.

May Kogi State continue to enjoy peace, unity, progress, food security, economic growth and sustainable development.

Once again, congratulations to the Government and people of Kogi State on 35 years of shared history, resilience and progress.

HAPPY 35TH ANNIVERSARY, KOGI STATE!
