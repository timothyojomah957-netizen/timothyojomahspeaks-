HON. TIMOTHY OJOMAH COMMENDS DEMOCRATIC PARTICIPATION AS LOCAL GOVERNMENT ELECTION HOLDS IN IBAJI AND ACROSS KOGI STATE

By IBAJI TV Channel | 17 October 2026

Hon. Timothy Ojomah, Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security in Kogi State, has commended the participation of citizens in the local government election in Ibaji and across Kogi State.

The election provided an opportunity for eligible citizens to exercise their democratic rights and participate in the process of choosing leaders at the grassroots level. The importance of peaceful participation, unity, and responsible civic engagement remains central to strengthening democracy and promoting development in our communities.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah's message highlights the importance of democratic values, peaceful coexistence, and collective responsibility in building a stronger and more united society. Local government remains an important level of governance because it brings leadership closer to the people and provides an opportunity to address community needs.

Ibaji Local Government Area and other communities across Kogi State stand to benefit when citizens, community leaders, and public office holders work together in the interest of peace, progress, and development. The continued participation of citizens in democratic processes can help strengthen accountability and encourage effective grassroots governance.

As Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Hon. Timothy Ojomah also serves in a sector that is important to the welfare of citizens. Agriculture contributes to food availability, employment, household income, and economic development. Sustained cooperation between government and communities is important to improving agricultural opportunities and supporting food security across the state.

Beyond the election, the need for unity and peaceful coexistence remains important. Citizens are encouraged to respect one another, maintain peace, and work together regardless of political differences. The progress of Ibaji and Kogi State depends on the shared commitment of the people to development, stability, and the common good.

The local government election also provides an opportunity to reflect on the responsibilities of elected leaders and the expectations of the people. Leadership at every level should be guided by accountability, service, transparency, and a genuine commitment to improving the lives of citizens.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah's commendation serves as a reminder that democracy is strengthened when citizens participate peacefully and when leaders remain committed to serving their communities. The collective goal should be a peaceful, united, and prosperous Ibaji and Kogi State.
