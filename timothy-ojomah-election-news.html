HON. TIMOTHY OJOMAH COMMENDS DEMOCRATIC PARTICIPATION AS LOCAL GOVERNMENT ELECTION HOLDS IN IBAJI AND ACROSS KOGI STATE

By IBAJI TV Channel | 17 October 2026

Hon. Timothy Ojomah has commended democratic participation as the local government election takes place in Ibaji and across Kogi State, emphasizing the importance of peace, unity, responsible citizenship and grassroots development.

The local government election represents an important opportunity for eligible citizens to exercise their democratic rights and participate in choosing leaders at the grassroots level. Peaceful participation and respect for democratic processes are essential to building a stronger society.

Hon. Timothy Ojomah's message highlights the importance of democratic values, peaceful coexistence and collective responsibility in promoting progress across Ibaji Local Government Area and the wider Kogi State. Local government remains a vital level of governance because it brings leadership closer to the people and provides an opportunity to address the needs of communities.

The progress of Ibaji and other communities across Kogi State depends on cooperation between citizens, community leaders and public office holders. When people work together in the interest of peace and development, communities are better positioned to pursue lasting progress.

As Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Hon. Timothy Ojomah serves in a sector that plays an important role in the welfare and economic wellbeing of citizens. Agriculture supports food availability, employment, household income and economic growth. Continued attention to agricultural development and food security remains important for communities across the state.

Beyond the election, peaceful coexistence and unity remain essential. Citizens are encouraged to respect one another and maintain peace regardless of political differences. The common goal should be to build a society where development, security and the wellbeing of the people receive priority.

Local government leadership also carries the responsibility of serving the people with accountability, transparency and dedication. Citizens naturally look to their leaders for solutions to community challenges and for initiatives that can improve everyday life.

Democratic participation is most meaningful when it is accompanied by responsible leadership, respect for the rule of law and a shared commitment to the common good. A peaceful and united Ibaji, alongside a prosperous Kogi State, remains an important aspiration for all who desire sustainable development.
