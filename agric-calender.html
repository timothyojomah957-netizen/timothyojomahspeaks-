Agricultural Calendar

A general guide to agricultural activities and seasonal planning for farmers and agricultural stakeholders in Kogi State.

General Farming Calendar

Agricultural activities depend on rainfall, location, crop variety, soil conditions and farming practices. The calendar below is a general planning guide and should be adjusted according to local conditions.

Period Major Activity Examples Planning Focus
January – February Farm Preparation Land planning, equipment maintenance Prepare inputs and farming plans
March – April Early Season Preparation Land clearing and seed preparation Prepare for planting season
May – June Planting Crop planting and establishment Timely planting and crop management
July – August Crop Management Weeding, pest monitoring and field management Protect and maintain crops
September – October Harvest Period Harvesting and post-harvest handling Reduce losses and maintain quality
November – December Post-Harvest Storage, marketing and farm evaluation Prepare for the next production cycle

Key Seasonal Activities

🌱 Land Preparation

Farmers can use the pre-planting period to prepare farmland, equipment, seeds and other necessary inputs.

🌾 Planting

Planting should be planned according to rainfall patterns, crop type, soil conditions and recommended agricultural practices.

💧 Crop Management

Regular field monitoring, weed control, appropriate water management and pest monitoring are important during crop development.

🌽 Harvesting

Harvesting at the appropriate stage can help maintain product quality and reduce agricultural losses.

📦 Storage

Proper storage and handling can help protect harvested agricultural products from damage and unnecessary losses.

🛒 Marketing

Farmers can plan ahead for market opportunities, transportation and sales of agricultural products.

Important Notice

This calendar is a general agricultural planning guide. Actual farming dates may vary because of rainfall, climate, crop type and local conditions. Farmers should seek current agricultural advice before making production decisions.
