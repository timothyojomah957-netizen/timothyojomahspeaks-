COMMISSIONER'S PORTFOLIO

Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security, Kogi State

Agricultural Development

Promoting agricultural development across Kogi State by supporting farmers, encouraging modern farming methods and improving agricultural productivity.

Food Security

Supporting programmes designed to increase food production and strengthen food security for communities across Kogi State.

Farmer Support

Working with farmers and agricultural organisations to improve access to agricultural inputs, knowledge, technology and opportunities.

Agricultural Mechanisation

Encouraging the use of modern agricultural equipment and mechanisation to improve efficiency, productivity and the scale of farming activities.

Youth Empowerment

Encouraging young people to participate in agriculture by promoting agricultural skills, entrepreneurship and employment opportunities.

Agricultural Value Chains

Supporting the development of agricultural value chains that connect farmers with processing, markets, investment and other opportunities.

Agricultural Investment

Promoting opportunities for private-sector investment and partnerships that can strengthen agriculture and create sustainable economic opportunities in Kogi State.

Building a Stronger Agricultural Future

The Ministry remains focused on building a productive, sustainable and inclusive agricultural sector that benefits farmers, young people, businesses and communities throughout Kogi State.
