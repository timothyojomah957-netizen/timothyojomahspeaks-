Agricultural Standards & Quality Control

Promoting good agricultural, processing, storage and handling practices that support the quality, safety and marketability of agricultural products in Kogi State.

Quality in Agricultural Production

Quality management is important throughout the agricultural value chain. From production and harvesting to processing, storage and marketing, appropriate practices can help protect the quality of agricultural products.

🌱

Good Agricultural Practices

Encouraging appropriate farming practices that support productive, responsible and quality-oriented agricultural production.

🔬

Quality Monitoring

Supporting awareness and monitoring of agricultural product quality at relevant stages of the value chain.

🧪

Product Safety

Promoting practices that help reduce contamination and protect the safety and integrity of agricultural and food products.

📦

Proper Packaging

Appropriate packaging can help protect products from damage and contamination during storage and transportation.

🏪

Market Quality

Good quality practices can help agricultural products meet market expectations and improve their competitiveness.

📋

Record Keeping

Proper records can support traceability, accountability and better management of agricultural production and processing activities.

Key Quality Areas

Clean Production

Maintaining clean production and handling environments can help protect agricultural products.

Safe Handling

Appropriate handling during harvesting, transportation and storage can reduce contamination and physical damage.

Proper Storage

Suitable storage conditions can help maintain product quality and reduce post-harvest losses.

Processing Hygiene

Good hygiene practices are important when agricultural products are processed into food and other value-added products.

Packaging Standards

Suitable packaging can help protect products and communicate important information to buyers and consumers.

Traceability

Appropriate records can help identify the source and movement of agricultural products through the value chain.

Good Quality Practices

1. Use Quality Inputs

Farmers and agricultural businesses should use appropriate inputs obtained through reliable and authorized channels.

2. Maintain Hygiene

Production, processing and storage areas should be maintained in suitable hygienic conditions.

3. Handle Products Carefully

Proper harvesting, loading, transportation and storage practices can help maintain product quality.

4. Keep Accurate Records

Records of production, processing and movement can support quality management and accountability.

5. Follow Applicable Requirements

Agricultural businesses should follow applicable laws, regulations and standards relevant to their products and activities.

Important Notice: Specific standards, certification requirements and regulatory procedures may vary according to the agricultural product and activity. Producers and businesses should obtain current guidance from the appropriate government and regulatory authorities.

Promoting Quality Agriculture

Strong quality-control practices can improve consumer confidence, protect agricultural products and help farmers and businesses access better markets.
