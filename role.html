Hon. Timothy Ojomah and Ibaji

Hon. Timothy Ojomah is the Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security in Kogi State. According to the Kogi State Government's official biography, his roots trace back to Ibaji Local Government Area.

Before becoming Commissioner for Agriculture, he served in Ibaji Local Government in leadership positions, including Supervisory Councillor for Works and Housing and Acting Caretaker Chairman.

His experience at the grassroots level provides an important connection between agricultural policy at the state level and the realities faced by rural communities and farmers.

Agriculture and Opportunities in Ibaji

Ibaji is part of agricultural programmes operating in Kogi State. The IFAD-assisted Value Chain Development Programme has included Ibaji among participating local government areas for rice and cassava value-chain support.

  • Promotion of rice and cassava production.
  • Farmer training and capacity development.
  • Access to agricultural inputs and equipment through government and development programmes.
  • Development of agricultural value chains and market opportunities.
  • Encouragement of youth and women participation in agriculture.
  • Strengthening agriculture as a source of employment, income and food security.

From Grassroots Experience to Agricultural Leadership

Hon. Timothy Ojomah's previous service in Ibaji Local Government gave him experience in grassroots administration and community development before his appointment as Commissioner for Agriculture.

His official profile also records his experience as a board member of the Kogi State Agro-Allied Company, further strengthening his connection with agricultural development and food systems.

As Commissioner, his responsibilities extend across the entire state, including policies and programmes intended to improve agricultural productivity, farmer empowerment, mechanisation and food security.

Agricultural Transformation

Under the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security, programmes have focused on increasing food production, improving access to agricultural inputs and strengthening agricultural value chains.

The Ministry has also participated in initiatives involving farmer training, improved market access and agricultural machinery. These efforts are intended to make agriculture more productive and create stronger economic opportunities for farming communities.

Vision for Ibaji

A stronger Ibaji where farmers have access to productive farmland, modern agricultural technology, financing, quality inputs, infrastructure and reliable markets; enabling agriculture to become a stronger foundation for food security, employment and community development.
