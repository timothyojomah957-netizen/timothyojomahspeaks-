Hon. Timothy Ojomah Speaks on the 2027 Idah Federal Constituency Election

September 2026

The Honourable Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, Hon. Timothy Ojomah, has expressed confidence regarding the forthcoming 2027 election in the Idah Federal Constituency.

Speaking during an interaction monitored by Ibaji TV Channel, the Commissioner shared his views on the political developments and the prospects surrounding the forthcoming election.

Hon. Ojomah emphasized the importance of peace, unity and responsible participation among the people as preparations for the election continue.

He encouraged the people to remain peaceful and engaged in the democratic process, while stressing the importance of purposeful representation, development and the welfare of constituents.

The Commissioner expressed his confidence in the candidate he supports, while the eventual outcome of the election will be determined through the democratic electoral process.
